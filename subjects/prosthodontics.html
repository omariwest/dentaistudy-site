Prosthodontics Study Hub

A structured prosthodontics map for crowns, bridges, RPDs, complete dentures, occlusion, implants, impressions, margins, ferrule decisions, and common prosthetic complications.

How to use this hub

Do not study prosthodontics as separate appliances. Most exam cases follow one clinical path: assess restorability and occlusion, choose the simplest suitable prosthesis, protect biology, design retention and support, then manage complications.

1. Restore or replace Ferrule, crown margin, post/core, endocrown, bridge, implant, or RPD.
2. Design support Occlusion, retention, stability, rests, guide planes, denture extension, and impressions.
3. Manage failure Loose temporary crowns, open margins, bridge risk, denture instability, and material choice.
