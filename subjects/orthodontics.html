Orthodontics Study Hub

A structured orthodontics map for diagnosis, cephalometrics, problem lists, space analysis, malocclusion classification, growth modification, crowding, expansion, biomechanics, anchorage, impacted canines, and retention.

How to use this hub

Do not study orthodontics as appliance names only. In exams, most cases follow one clinical path: diagnose the skeletal, dental, soft tissue, and functional problems; calculate the space need; choose timing and mechanics; then justify retention and relapse control.

1. Diagnose the case Ceph, classification, problem list, space analysis, and growth status.
2. Choose the movement Expansion, IPR, extraction, growth modification, camouflage, surgery, or interceptive care.
3. Control stability Anchorage, torque, bite correction, compliance, retention, and relapse risk.
