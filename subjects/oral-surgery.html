Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery Study Hub

A structured oral surgery map for extraction decisions, third molars, infections, bleeding, trauma, medical risk, and post-operative complications.

How to use this hub

Do not study oral surgery as a list of operations. Most exam cases follow one clinical path: assess risk, decide whether to treat or refer, control infection or bleeding, then explain the complication and follow-up plan clearly.

1. Risk first Medical history, anticoagulants, MRONJ, ORN, and antibiotic need.
2. Procedure decision Simple extraction, surgical extraction, third molar removal, coronectomy, or referral.
3. Complication control Bleeding, dry socket, infection spread, nerve injury, OAC, and trauma triage.
