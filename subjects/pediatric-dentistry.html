Pediatric Dentistry Study Hub

A structured pediatric dentistry map for child-patient exams: behavior, pulp therapy, caries control, trauma, eruption, space management, and safe clinical decision-making.

How to use this hub

Do not study pediatric dentistry as separate facts. In exams, most cases follow one clinical path: gain cooperation, diagnose the tooth, choose pulp or extraction, restore safely, protect space, then plan follow-up with the parent.

1. Cooperation Behavior guidance and local anesthesia safety.
2. Tooth decision IPT, pulpotomy, pulpectomy, extraction, or immature permanent tooth care.
3. Child-level plan Caries control, crowns, space maintainers, trauma review, and parent advice.
