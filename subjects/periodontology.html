Periodontology Study Hub

A structured periodontology map for diagnosis, staging and grading, periodontal charting, non-surgical therapy, re-evaluation, furcation, recession, surgery, implants, and maintenance.

How to use this hub

Do not study periodontology as pocket numbers only. Most exam cases follow one clinical path: screen, chart fully when needed, diagnose stage and grade, control risk factors, treat non-surgically, then re-evaluate before deciding maintenance, surgery, or referral.

1. Diagnose clearly BPE/PSR, full charting, BOP, pocket depth, CAL, bone loss, stage, and grade.
2. Treat risk first OHI, plaque control, smoking, diabetes, SRP, re-evaluation, and residual pockets.
3. Escalate properly Maintenance, flap surgery, regeneration, furcation care, recession treatment, or referral.
