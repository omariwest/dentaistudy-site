About

Built for dental students who study under pressure

DentAIstudy helps you move from dental material to clearer understanding, active recall, and saved study work.

Our mission

DentAIstudy was built to make dental revision feel less scattered. Instead of moving between PDFs, notes, search tabs, and generic AI chats, students can use one focused workspace for notes, explanations, practice, and saved revision.

Why we built it

Dental students do not only need more information. They need a cleaner way to turn dense material into study work they can use before exams, clinics, viva sessions, and board preparation.

  • Turn dental PDFs and lecture files into professional notes
  • Use Exam Coach when a topic needs a focused explanation
  • Create flashcards and quizzes from weak topics
  • Keep saved notes, chats, decks, and quizzes inside Library

What DentAIstudy helps you do

  • Study from your own dental PDFs, files, and weak topics
  • Generate cleaner notes for revision
  • Ask focused dental questions with Exam Coach
  • Practice with flashcards and quizzes
  • Reopen saved work from your Library

What DentAIstudy is not

DentAIstudy is study support, not clinical advice, a pass guarantee, or a replacement for official exam guidance. Always verify important content with your school, textbooks, supervisors, and trusted clinical references.

What we care about

DentAIstudy is designed to reduce study friction, protect trust, and help dental students practice more actively.

Exam realism

We build around how dental students revise for OSCE, viva, boards, clinical exams, and subject finals, not generic study prompts.

Cognitive simplicity

Less clutter, less jumping between tabs. The goal is to help you move from material to understanding to practice faster.

Clarity over hype

We keep the promise honest: DentAIstudy can support better studying, but your results still come from your effort, judgment, and proper revision.
