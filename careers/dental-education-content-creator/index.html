Job Role

Dental Education Content Creator

Create clear, practical content that shows dental students and exam candidates how DentAIstudy can support everyday study and exam preparation.

Remote Flexible Commission based

About the opportunity

DentAIstudy is seeking people with a genuine connection to dental education who can create clear, useful content showing how the platform fits into real study routines. Content should be practical, accurate, and relevant to dental students and exam candidates.

What you’ll do

  • Create short form videos and educational content that demonstrate practical DentAIstudy workflows.
  • Show how features such as PDF study, notes, flashcards, quizzes, and Exam Coach can support different study needs.
  • Publish through your own social channels and present DentAIstudy accurately and professionally.
  • Develop content relevant to dental study, exam preparation, and student life in a style suitable for your audience.

Who we’re looking for

  • Dental students, dental graduates, dentists, postgraduate residents, or dental exam candidates.
  • People comfortable creating content for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, or similar platforms.
  • Follower count is not a deciding factor. We are interested in clear, useful content and an audience that is relevant to dental education.
  • Applications are welcome from any country. Experience creating content for candidates preparing for INBDE, ADC, ORE, NDECC, ADEX, or any other dental examination is a plus.

What matters to us

  • Clear and professional communication.
  • Honest representation of DentAIstudy.
  • Content that is genuinely useful to dental students rather than copied promotional material.
  • Consistency, reliability, and willingness to learn what resonates with your audience.

Compensation and working arrangement

The role is remote and flexible. Compensation is commission based and linked to qualifying subscriptions generated through your assigned creator attribution. Full commercial terms will be provided to selected applicants before acceptance.

This is an independent commission based creator opportunity, not a salaried employment position. There is no fee or purchase required to apply or participate.

Interested in this role?

Complete the application form and attach your CV. Applications will be assessed against the requirements of the role, and selected candidates will be contacted directly.
