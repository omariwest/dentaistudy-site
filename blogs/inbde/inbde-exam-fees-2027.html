INBDE Exam Fees 2027: Application and Rescheduling Costs

The JCNDE had not yet published a separate 2027 INBDE Candidate Guide as of 17 November 2026. The current official guide is the 2026 INBDE Candidate Guide, updated 4 August 2026, and it lists an individual INBDE examination fee of $890 USD.

That is the correct current figure for planning, but it should not be presented as a guaranteed 2027 price before JCNDE publishes or confirms the fee schedule that will apply to your application date.

International dental students and graduates from non-CODA-accredited programs also pay a $435 processing fee, bringing the current JCNDE application cost to $1,325 USD for a typical international candidate taking the INBDE alone.

Quick Answers

  1. Current INBDE examination fee: $890 USD.
  2. International processing fee: $435 USD for students or graduates of non-CODA-accredited dental programs.
  3. Current calculated international total: $1,325 USD for the INBDE application plus international processing, before optional services.
  4. Eligibility extension: $150 for one 45-day extension under the current guide.
  5. Additional result report: $55 per recipient not selected at application.
  6. Result-audit fee: the August 2026 Candidate Guide lists $105, while the current JCNDE results webpage still displays $65. Verify the live amount in your DENTPIN account when requesting an audit.

Examination Fee: $890

The current 2026 Candidate Guide lists $890 for an INBDE administration purchased individually.

The fee includes:

  • the candidate's official result;
  • reporting to up to three licensing jurisdictions when selected at application;
  • reporting to the candidate's CODA-accredited dental program where applicable.

JCNDE states that examination fees are non-refundable and non-transferable.

This is why candidates should not submit an application simply to inspect available Prometric dates. Once the application is submitted and processed, the fee is committed and the eligibility period begins.

International Candidate Processing Fee: $435

If you are a student or graduate of a dental program that is not accredited by CODA, the current guide adds a $435 international processing fee.

Calculated current application total:

$890 + $435 = $1,325 USD

That calculation does not include:

  • credential-related costs outside JCNDE;
  • travel to the Prometric centre;
  • rescheduling;
  • a result audit;
  • extra result reports;
  • a future re-examination if you do not pass.

See INBDE International Dentist Path for the broader pathway.

Optional INBDE + DLOSCE Bundle

The current Candidate Guide lists an INBDE and DLOSCE bundle for $1,080 USD at the time of application.

The guide states this is optional. Candidates can still purchase the examinations individually.

Do not buy the bundle simply because the discount exists. First confirm whether DLOSCE is relevant to the licensing jurisdiction and pathway you are pursuing.

For more context, see INBDE + DLOSCE Bundle.

Eligibility Extension Fee: $150

After DTS processes the application, the normal eligibility period is generally six months.

The current guide permits one 45-day extension per application for $150.

You cannot have a currently scheduled examination appointment when submitting the extension request. If you have an appointment, you need to cancel it first.

The extension fee is non-refundable and non-transferable.

If you let the eligibility period end without testing or an approved extension, you can lose the original examination fee and need a new application.

Prometric Rescheduling Costs

Current JCNDE scheduling guidance lists:

NoticeFee
30 or more business days$50
5–29 business days$70
1–4 business days$150
No-show or 30+ minutes late and denied admissionFull testing fee forfeited

You must reschedule through Prometric. Leaving a voicemail for a local test centre is not sufficient.

Your new appointment must still fall inside the eligibility period.

Result Report Fee: $55

At application, the exam fee includes result reporting to a limited number of recipients under the current guide.

If you later request a result report for a recipient that was not selected at application, the current fee is $55 per report.

Licensure requirements vary by jurisdiction, so decide which boards need the result before buying unnecessary reports.

Result Audit: Official Fee Conflict

The most recent candidate guide available as of our verification date—updated 4 August 2026—lists the result-audit fee as $105.

However, JCNDE's current public INBDE results page still says $65.

Both are official JCNDE sources, so there is an unresolved website inconsistency.

The safe rule for a 2027 candidate is:

  • treat $105 as the newer guide figure for budgeting;
  • verify the actual fee displayed in your DENTPIN account at the moment you submit an audit request;
  • do not rely on an old screenshot or a third-party article.

An audit checks result accuracy; it is not a re-evaluation of the examination content.

What Happens Financially If You Fail?

A new application and examination fee are required for each new attempt.

Current retest rules require:

  • at least 60 days between unsuccessful INBDE attempts;
  • no more than four administrations in any 12-month period;
  • compliance with the JCNDE Five Years/Five Attempts rule.

Under the current guide, candidates must pass within five years of the first attempt or within five attempts, whichever comes first. After the fifth year or fifth unsuccessful attempt, the current August 2026 guide says a candidate may test once every six months after the most recent attempt.

See INBDE Retake Strategy.

2027 Fee Caution

Because no separate 2027 Candidate Guide was available on 17 November 2026, these should be described as current fees for 2027 planning, not guaranteed future fees.

Before submitting a 2027 application:

  1. open the current INBDE Candidate Guide;
  2. check the JCNDE INBDE page;
  3. review the amount displayed before payment;
  4. use the live Prometric rescheduling schedule if you later change an appointment.

Common Cost Mistakes

  • Assuming the $890 fee is the total for an international graduate.
  • Forgetting the $435 international processing fee.
  • Applying before you are ready to use the six-month eligibility window.
  • Missing the eligibility window and forfeiting the application.
  • Treating the $150 extension as unlimited.
  • Rescheduling late and paying the highest Prometric fee.
  • Assuming an audit means your exam will be regraded.
  • Quoting a “2027 fee” before JCNDE has published a 2027 guide.

FAQs

1. How much is the INBDE for an international dentist?

Under the current August 2026 guide, the $890 examination fee plus $435 international processing fee equals $1,325 USD before optional services.

2. Are INBDE fees refundable?

JCNDE states that examination fees are non-refundable and non-transferable.

3. How much does it cost to reschedule?

The current JCNDE page lists $50 with 30+ business days' notice, $70 with 5–29 days and $150 with 1–4 business days.

4. How much is a result audit?

The August 2026 Candidate Guide lists $105, but the current JCNDE results page still displays $65. Verify the live amount when requesting the audit.

5. Are these guaranteed 2027 prices?

No. They are the current official fees available on 17 November 2026. JCNDE had not yet published a separate 2027 Candidate Guide.
