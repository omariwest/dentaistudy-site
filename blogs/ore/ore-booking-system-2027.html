ORE Booking 2027: MyGDC, Waiting for Seats and Cancellations

Securing a place on the General Dental Council (GDC) Overseas Registration Exam (ORE) is often just as competitive as sitting the exam itself. Under the GDC's delivery contract with UCL Consultants Ltd , all general ORE bookings run through your online MyGDC account, where demand routinely outstrips capacity within minutes of a window opening—the GDC notes that all places have been taken within 30 minutes of opening.

Quick Answers: How ORE Booking Works in 2027

Where do I book an ORE sitting?

(Official GDC Fact) All bookings and fee payments are completed online through your MyGDC account (the GDC announced that a revised candidate portal and booking system should be introduced for exams scheduled in 2027, but the live booking guidance still uses the current GDC online account).

How far in advance do ORE booking windows open?

(Official GDC Fact) As a general rule, the GDC opens ORE sittings for booking eight weeks before the examination date, announcing the exact date and UK-time opening timestamp by email and on its official ORE booking dates webpage.

Can I log in on my phone and laptop at the same time to book faster?

(Official GDC Fact) No. You may log in to your MyGDC account on only one device at a time. Inactivity for 15 minutes automatically logs you out, and if you are already logged in when the opening clock hits zero, you must refresh your browser to activate the booking button.

How long do I have to pay once I select a seat?

(Official GDC Fact) You must pay the full examination fee (£485 for Part 1; £6,967 for Part 2) at the time of booking—any booking not paid within one hour is cancelled, and clicking "book exam and pay" without intending to sit or pay blocks you from booking the next sitting.

Does the GDC maintain a waiting list when an exam sells out?

(Official GDC Fact) No. If booked candidates withdraw and free up spaces before candidate names are sent to the exam provider, the GDC reopens the booking window and offers those places openly to all eligible candidates via email notice and website update.

Who receives priority booking access?

(Official GDC Fact) Priority places are allocated ahead of the general first-come, first-served queue to candidates approaching the five-year limit for Part 2 and to dental professionals with refugee status (up to two attempts at Part 1 and two attempts at Part 2).

Candidate-List Eligibility & How Booking Windows Are Announced

1. Why You Must Be on the Candidate List First

Creating a MyGDC login alone does not let you see or book ORE sittings. You are only eligible to book once the GDC completes its qualification, clinical experience, and English language checks (currently taking around 60 working days) and emails you confirming your addition to the ORE Part 1 candidate list. Similarly, Part 2 bookings remain invisible until your Part 1 pass is issued and your profile moves to the ORE Part 2 candidate list.

2. GDC's "eight weeks before" Rule vs. Calculated Dates

GDC policy states that ORE sittings open for booking eight weeks in advance. However, candidates should never treat an eight-week backward calculation as an official opening date:

  • Calculated Dates Are Estimates Only: Counting back exactly 56 days from a confirmed exam date only tells you the approximate week to watch your inbox. The GDC sets the exact day and UK clock time operationally and publishes it on gdc-uk.org/registration/overseas-registration-exam/ore-booking-dates and by email.
  • Published 2027 Example vs. Unannounced Windows: as of November 18, 2026, the GDC's official ORE booking dates page confirms that booking for the 21–24 January 2027 ORE Part 2 sitting (200 places) opens on Tuesday 24 November 2026 at 14:30 (UK time)—roughly eight weeks and two days before Day 1. Meanwhile, while the GDC has confirmed 2027 exam dates for Part 1 (2–3 February 2027 and 6–7 April 2027) and Part 2 (4–7 March 2027 and 15–18 April 2027), the exact booking-opening dates and timestamps for those later 2027 sittings have not yet been published. Never trust third-party social-media rumours claiming unannounced March or April opening dates; rely strictly on GDC emails and the official ORE booking dates page.

MyGDC Technical Rules & Payment Requirements on Booking Day

For the current £115 / £485 / £6,967 / £686 charges and refund context, see ORE Exam Fees 2027.

When a booking window opens, traffic spikes instantaneously. The GDC enforces strict technical safeguards to keep access fair and prevent seat hoarding:

  • One-Device Login Rule: You can log in to your MyGDC account using only one device at a time. Opening MyGDC simultaneously on a laptop, tablet, and smartphone can trigger session conflicts or kick you out during checkout.
  • 15-Minute Inactivity Timeout: MyGDC automatically logs you out after 15 minutes of inactivity. If you log in too early and leave the page untouched, you risk being logged out right as the window opens.
  • UK Time & The 8-Hour 'Unavailable' Preview: All times published by the GDC are in UK time (Greenwich Mean Time in winter; British Summer Time from late March to late October). Eight hours before booking opens, the sitting appears in your MyGDC Bookings tab labeled 'unavailable' along with its exact opening time. If you are already logged in when that opening time arrives, you must refresh your browser to enable booking.
  • Strict One-Hour Payment Window: You must pay the full exam fee (£485 for Part 1 or £6,967 for Part 2; see ORE Exam Fees 2027) online when booking. Your booking will not be processed if payment is not completed within one hour. If a family member or third party is paying on your behalf, they must be ready to complete payment within that one-hour window—and remember the GDC warns candidates never to share account login credentials with third parties or study groups.
  • Penalty for Unpaid Seat Blocking: The GDC explicitly warns that if you select "book exam and pay" with no intention of sitting the exam or making payment, you will be prevented from booking onto the next available sitting.
  • Refugee Charity Cheque Exception: Cheque payments are permitted only for candidates sponsored by a refugee council or charity who contact the GDC Examinations team before the booking window opens to arrange the manual procedure.

Priority Booking vs. General Queue: Five-Year Limit & Refugee Rules

The two priority routes are explained in ORE Priority Booking 2027, including the five-year-limit and refugee arrangements.

After priority places are allocated, all remaining seats are released strictly first-come, first-served. Two candidate groups qualify for priority booking access (for full eligibility criteria, see our ORE Booking System Priority Rules guide):

  1. Candidates Nearing the Five-Year Limit (Part 2): Under GDC regulations, you must pass ORE Part 2 within five years of first attempting ORE Part 1. Because high demand could otherwise prevent a final-year candidate from securing a required retake, the GDC allocates priority Part 2 places to candidates approaching their five-year deadline and contacts them in advance of the booking window opening.
  2. Dental Professionals With Refugee Status: Candidates who hold refugee status may request priority booking access for up to two attempts at Part 1 and up to two attempts at Part 2. You do not need to upload refugee documentation during your initial ORE application; once you are added to the candidate list, contact the GDC Examinations team so they can verify your status and explain how to access priority booking. Priority status grants booking access ahead of the general rush, though candidates must still meet the exact same clinical and academic pass standards.

Waiting for Seats, Withdrawals & Reopened Booking Windows

If you miss out during the initial booking rush, you are not automatically locked out of that sitting—provided you understand how cancellations work:

  • What "Exam Full" Means: Because hundreds of candidates click simultaneously at opening, MyGDC can take several minutes to reconcile seat counts. Seeing an error message during those first few minutes usually indicates that the final places have just been claimed. Once all places are allocated, the Bookings tab displays 'closed – examination full' for two hours.
  • No Automated Waiting List: The GDC does not maintain a passive waitlist that automatically assigns cancelled seats to the next person in line.
  • How Withdrawals Trigger Reopened Windows: Under the GDC's withdrawal policy, if a booked candidate formally withdraws before the GDC submits candidate names to the examination provider (typically around five weeks before the exam), that vacated place returns to the pool. Whenever places become free due to withdrawals, the GDC reopens the booking window and offers those spaces openly to all eligible candidates.
  • How to Catch Reopened Seats: The GDC announces reopened windows by email and posts updates on the ORE booking dates webpage. Because reopened windows often release only a handful of cancelled places, having your payment card ready and checking email notifications promptly is essential.

Practical Candidate Scenarios for 2027

Scenario 1: Overseas Part 1 Candidate Booking From a Different Time Zone

  • Situation: You are added to the Part 1 candidate list and want to sit one of the confirmed 2027 Part 1 dates (2–3 February 2027 or 6–7 April 2027, 600 places each).
  • Action Plan: Because GDC has not yet published the exact booking timestamps for those 2027 sittings, watch your email starting ~9–10 weeks before each exam. Once GDC announces the UK opening time, convert it carefully to your local time zone (accounting for GMT vs. BST), notify your bank in advance of an international £485 GBP charge, log in on a single laptop 5–10 minutes early, and refresh at the exact UK opening minute.

Scenario 2: Part 2 Candidate Targeting the January 21–24, 2027 Sitting

  • Situation: You are on the Part 2 candidate list and plan to book the 21–24 January 2027 sitting (200 places at UCL Eastman Dental Institute).
  • Action Plan: GDC's ORE booking dates page confirms booking opens on Tuesday 24 November 2026 at 14:30 (UK time). Log in on one device shortly after 14:15 UK time (after the 'unavailable' preview has been visible since 06:30 UK time), refresh at exactly 14:30 UK time, and complete your £6,967 payment within one hour.

Scenario 3: Candidate Who Missed the Initial Window and Hopes for Cancellations

  • Situation: Every seat fills within 30 minutes of opening, and your Bookings tab displays 'closed – examination full'.
  • Action Plan: Keep £485 or £6,967 readily accessible in your account and enable instant push alerts for @gdc-uk.org emails. If a booked candidate withdraws before candidate lists are locked with UCL Consultants (~5 weeks before exam day), GDC will email eligible candidates and update ORE booking dates to reopen booking for those freed places.

Why Non-Refundable Travel Must Wait Until Confirmation

Whether you live in the UK or are flying to London from overseas, never book non-refundable flights, rail tickets, or hotels before your ORE place is confirmed:

  1. Payment & Allocation Confirmation First: A seat is only secured once your online fee payment completes within the one-hour window and MyGDC confirms your booking. For Part 1, the exam provider subsequently contacts candidates directly to confirm which of the three London venues (Teamco on Gray's Inn Road, Escalla in Kentish Town, or Tobacco Dock in Wapping) you have been assigned.
  2. No Fee Refund for UK Visa Delays: Under the GDC's ORE withdrawal policy, candidates are solely responsible for ensuring they have legal permission to enter the UK before booking. Being unable to obtain a UK visa does not qualify as an exceptional circumstance for a fee refund—meaning a visa delay after booking a £6,967 Part 2 seat can cost you the entire examination fee.

Common ORE Booking Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Logging Into MyGDC on Multiple Devices Simultaneously: Using a phone and laptop at the same time violates GDC's one-device rule and risks session errors during checkout.
  2. Forgetting to Refresh the Browser at Opening Time: Sitting on the 'unavailable' screen past the UK opening timestamp without hitting refresh leaves the booking button disabled.
  3. Treating an 8-Week Calendar Calculation as a Guaranteed Opening Date: Always wait for GDC's official email and ORE booking dates posting rather than assuming an exact 56-day countdown.
  4. Clicking "Book Exam and Pay" Without Ready Funds: Failing to complete payment within one hour voids your booking, and testing the checkout flow without intending to pay triggers a ban from the next sitting.
  5. Sharing MyGDC Login Details With Friends or Agents: GDC explicitly prohibits sharing account credentials; doing so risks security breaches and disciplinary action under ORE rules.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. When do ORE booking windows open for 2027 exams?

(Official GDC Fact) As a general rule, ORE sittings open for booking eight weeks before the exam date, with exact dates and UK-time opening timestamps announced by email and on the GDC's ORE booking dates page. as of November 18, 2026, GDC has confirmed that booking for the 21–24 January 2027 Part 2 sitting opens on Tuesday 24 November 2026 at 14:30 (UK time); opening timestamps for later 2027 Part 1 and Part 2 sittings have not yet been published.

2. Why does MyGDC say 'unavailable' when I look at the Bookings tab?

(Official GDC Fact) An upcoming ORE sitting appears in your MyGDC Bookings tab as 'unavailable' exactly eight hours before it opens, alongside the scheduled UK opening time. Once that UK opening time arrives, refresh your browser to begin booking.

3. How long do I have to pay after selecting a seat in MyGDC?

(Official GDC Fact) You must pay the full examination fee (£485 for Part 1 or £6,967 for Part 2) online when booking, and your booking will not be processed if payment is not completed within one hour.

4. What happens if an ORE sitting sells out—is there a waiting list?

(Official GDC Fact) There is no waiting list. When all seats fill, MyGDC displays 'closed – examination full' for two hours. If places later open up due to candidate withdrawals before names are sent to the exam provider, the GDC notifies candidates by email, updates the ORE booking dates page, and reopens the booking window openly.

5. Who qualifies for priority booking on the ORE?

(Official GDC Fact) The GDC provides priority booking access to candidates approaching the five-year time limit to complete ORE Part 2 and to dental professionals with refugee status (for up to two attempts at Part 1 and two attempts at Part 2).
