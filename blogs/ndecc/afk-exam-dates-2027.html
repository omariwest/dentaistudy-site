AFK Exam Dates 2027: February, August & Registration Deadlines

Last verified against NDEB sources: 1 November 2026. The NDEB dates page was last modified by NDEB on 8 October 2026. Dates, seat availability and policies can change, so confirm in your NDEBConnect account before you plan travel or leave.

The NDEB has published two Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge (AFK) sittings for 2027: 5–6 February and 13 August. They are not equivalent. February has separate Canada and Australia/New Zealand dates and is already open for registration. August is listed for Canada only, and registration opens on 2 March 2027.

This article covers the calendar and the preparation timeline: dates, deadlines, time zones, delivery formats, what changed in registration, and how to work backwards from each sitting. It does not walk through the booking screens.

Quick Answers

  1. February 2027: Canada sits on Friday 5 February 2027. Australia and New Zealand sit on Saturday 6 February 2027. The registration deadline for both is 4 January 2027 at 21:00 ET, and registration was open at the time of verification.
  2. August 2027: Canada sits on Friday 13 August 2027. Registration opens 2 March 2027 at 10:00 ET and closes 12 July 2027 at 21:00 ET. No Australia/New Zealand August sitting was listed at the time of verification.
  3. All NDEB deadlines and opening times are in Eastern Time (ET). Convert them to your own time zone and do not leave registration to the last hour.
  4. You need an approved Equivalency Process application before you can take the AFK. Registration and payment happen in your NDEBConnect account.
  5. Seats can run out before the deadline. NDEB announced that additional February 2027 seats open on 22 December 2026 at 10:00 ET, and that a second registration period now opens about 6–8 weeks before each exam.
  6. Delivery is paper-based or electronic at Prometric. NDEB's pages do not say which format applies to which 2027 sitting, so check the instructions in your own registration.

For February, 22 December falls about 6½ weeks before 5 February, which is consistent with NDEB's "about 6–8 weeks" description of the second registration. NDEB's notice calls these "additional seats" and does not say which locations they apply to. Check your account.

In both sittings the gap between the registration deadline and the exam is about 32 days (4 January to 5 February; 12 July to 13 August). That gap is when electronic candidates receive their Prometric scheduling instructions, as explained below.

The February 2027 sitting

February is the nearer sitting and the only one with an Australia/New Zealand date. Three points matter for planning:

  • The deadline is firm, but seats may not last until it. The first registration period closes when all exam locations are full, according to NDEB's August 2026 announcement. NDEB's AFK page also says it is "not unusual for examinations to fill-up prior to the registration deadline." An "open" status on 1 November 2026 does not guarantee a seat on 15 December.
  • There is a scheduled second chance. Additional seats open on 22 December 2026 at 10:00 ET. This is the date to note if you could not get a seat in the first registration.
  • Canada and Australia/NZ sit one day apart. The Australia/NZ sitting is on 6 February, the day after the Canadian one. NDEB's pages do not publish exam start times, so confirm yours in your registration documents.

The August 2027 sitting

August has a longer runway. Registration opens on 2 March 2027 at 10:00 ET and closes on 12 July 2027 at 21:00 ET, a window of over four months. NDEB's August 2026 announcement says the first registration closes when all locations are full, so that window may end earlier in practice.

At verification, only a Canadian sitting (13 August) was listed. If you live in Australia or New Zealand and want to sit locally, do not assume an August date will appear. Watch NDEB's dates page, and treat the Canadian sitting or a later date as the confirmed options until NDEB publishes otherwise.

Eligibility and NDEB registration

The AFK sits inside the NDEB Equivalency Process, the pathway for graduates of dental programs not recognized by the Commission on Dental Accreditation of Canada (CDAC). NDEB states that once your Equivalency Process application is approved, you can take the AFK.

Three official requirements matter for your calendar:

  • Registration is by NDEBConnect. You log in to register and pay.
  • A pass is required before you progress. NDEB states that a passing grade on the AFK is required before you continue to the other examinations in the Equivalency Process.
  • There is a limit of three attempts. You can take the AFK a maximum of three times. A weak first attempt costs more than a delayed one.

The pass mark is a test-equated, re-scaled score of 75 or greater. Results include that score, and NDEB provides no further breakdown. The exam has 200 single-answer multiple-choice questions in two parts, with two hours for each part.

If your application is still being processed, you cannot register. Build your calendar around the approval date, not the exam date.

Paper vs electronic delivery and Prometric

NDEB describes the AFK as offered in two formats: electronic delivery at Prometric test centres and booklet format at select exam sites. The dates page adds that the paper-based version is administered across Canada, and the electronic version at Prometric centres within Canada and select international locations.

The pages do not state which format applies to the February or August 2027 sitting in each country. Your registration is the authority for your format.

If seats are unavailable: the two-period registration model

NDEB changed the AFK, ACJ and Virtual OSCE registration process effective 1 September 2026:

  • First registration opens on a set date and time and closes when all exam locations are full.
  • Second registration opens about 6–8 weeks before the exam. Candidates can register or transfer if seats become available through withdrawals or increased capacity.

Transfer rules are narrow. NDEB states that "candidates may transfer between exam locations only when registration is open," and that they may not transfer between registration periods.

Withdrawal fees also changed. Before the registration deadline, the fee is 50% of the exam fee. After the deadline, it is 100%. The fee for an approved withdrawal for medical or other unforeseen exceptional circumstances is unchanged. These fees apply to the AFK, ACJ and Virtual OSCE.

The announcement covers transfers between locations. It does not say how moving from the February sitting to the August one is handled, so ask NDEB before you assume a switch is possible.

Candidate planning examples

These timelines are illustrative study schedules, not NDEB guidance. Adjust them to your own baseline.

Example 1: Approved candidate, targeting February. Your application is approved and you want 5 or 6 February.

  • Register as early as you can. The first registration can close before 4 January.
  • If you miss the first round, set a reminder for 22 December 2026 at 10:00 ET, converted to your time zone.
  • Study backwards from the exam: baseline self-assessment about 12 weeks out, topic review through weeks 12–5, then timed two-part practice in the last four weeks.
  • If you are electronic, expect Prometric instructions only after 4 January.

Example 2: Application still pending in November. You cannot register until approval. A February seat is possible only if approval comes before the deadline and seats remain. Treat August as your main target. Registration opens 2 March, so use December to February for foundation study rather than waiting for a date.

Example 3: No seat in February. Check the 22 December additional seats and your account for the second registration. If neither works, plan for August. Only the Canadian sitting (13 August) was listed. The registration window runs from 2 March to 12 July, but expect the first registration to close earlier if locations fill.

Example 4: Australia/New Zealand candidate wanting August. No August sitting was listed for your region at verification. Do not book non-refundable plans on one. Watch NDEB's dates page, and decide in advance whether you would travel for the Canadian date.

FAQs

1. What are the AFK exam dates in 2027? NDEB lists 5 February (Canada), 6 February (Australia and New Zealand) and 13 August (Canada). Registration for February closes on 4 January 2027 at 21:00 ET. Registration for August opens on 2 March 2027 at 10:00 ET and closes on 12 July 2027 at 21:00 ET.

2. What time zone are the deadlines in? Eastern Time. A deadline of "21:00 ET" on 4 January 2027 falls on 5 January in much of Asia, Australia and New Zealand. Convert it using the table above, and register well ahead of it.

3. What if I cannot register because the exam is full? NDEB's AFK page says you can only register when registration is open and spaces are available. Seats can run out before the deadline. Use the second registration period, which opens about 6–8 weeks before the exam, and the February notice that extra seats open on 22 December 2026 at 10:00 ET.

4. Can I move my registration to a different location or sitting? NDEB allows transfers between exam locations only when registration is open, and not between registration periods. NDEB's announcement does not describe moving from one sitting to another. If you withdraw, the fee is 50% of the exam fee before the registration deadline and 100% after it, unless NDEB approves a medical or other exceptional circumstance. Ask NDEB before acting.

5. How many times can I take the AFK, and what is the pass mark? You can take it a maximum of three times. A test-equated, re-scaled score of 75 or greater is a pass.
