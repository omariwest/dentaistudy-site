AFK 2027 Format and Syllabus: Questions, Timing & Topics

The Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge (AFK) tests the biomedical and applied clinical science knowledge expected of a dentist entering the Canadian NDEB Equivalency Process. For 2027 candidates, the most useful official “syllabus” is not a commercial topic list—it is the current NDEB AFK blueprint and Knowledge, Skills and Abilities framework .

Quick Answers

How many AFK questions are there?

200.

Question type?

Single-answer multiple choice.

How long is the exam?

Four hours of examination time, divided into two 2-hour parts, with one scheduled break.

Computer or paper?

Both formats may be offered: Prometric computer-based delivery and booklet/paper format at selected sites.

Is there negative marking?

No. The current protocol says there is no penalty for an incorrect answer.

What is the passing standard?

A test-equated, re-scaled score of 75 or greater.

How many AFK attempts are allowed?

Three.

The Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge (AFK) tests the biomedical and applied clinical science knowledge expected of a dentist entering the Canadian NDEB Equivalency Process. For 2027 candidates, the most useful official “syllabus” is not a commercial topic list—it is the current NDEB AFK blueprint and Knowledge, Skills and Abilities framework.

As of 17 November 2026, the latest official document is the 2026 AFK Protocol effective January 19, 2026. NDEB has not published a separate 2027 AFK protocol. The current protocol specifies 200 single-answer multiple-choice questions, delivered in two parts of two hours each in a single day.

Current Protocol for 2027

NDEB's Exam Resources page currently lists:

2026 AFK Protocol — Revised

The protocol itself is effective January 19, 2026.

Until NDEB publishes a newer version, this is the official structure candidates should use for 2027 planning.

Always recheck the AFK page and Exam Resources page shortly before your examination because NDEB regularly updates questions as knowledge and professional standards change.

AFK 2027 Format

For the actual sitting calendar, use AFK Exam Dates 2027; for seat mechanics and deadlines, use AFK Registration 2027.

The examination is completed in one day.

Each question asks for the most likely or most appropriate answer.

Because there is no penalty for an incorrect response, the current protocol explicitly encourages candidates to answer every question even when uncertain.

“Syllabus” vs Official Blueprint

NDEB does not present AFK as a traditional university syllabus with a chapter-by-chapter reading list.

Instead, official examination items are developed from the Knowledge, Skills and Abilities (KSAs) for beginning dental practitioners in Canada.

The protocol then provides a blueprint showing the approximate proportion of questions from broad content areas.

That blueprint is the strongest official guide to what deserves study time.

What “Applied Biomedical Sciences” Includes

The current blueprint groups several foundational sciences together, including areas such as:

  • anatomy;
  • biochemistry;
  • biomaterials;
  • cariology;
  • growth and development;
  • histology;
  • immunology;
  • microbiology;
  • oral embryology;
  • physiology.

This is the largest blueprint area.

The practical implication is that an AFK preparation plan should not become only a collection of clinical treatment questions. Foundational biomedical knowledge remains a major part of the examination.

The Major Clinical Blocks

Three additional areas make up a large part of the blueprint:

Restorative, Prosthodontics and Implants

This cluster represents approximately 16 ± 5%.

Expect preparation to connect diagnosis and treatment principles with restorative dentistry, fixed/removable prosthodontics and implant-related concepts appropriate to an entry-to-practice dentist.

Oral Medicine/Oral Pathology and Oral Radiology

Approximately 15 ± 5%.

This area requires more than memorizing lesion names. Strong preparation includes recognizing clinical/radiographic findings and understanding their significance.

Pharmacology, Therapeutics, Local Anesthesia and Medical Emergencies

Approximately 14 ± 5%.

This combines medication knowledge with safe dental management and emergency readiness.

Together, these large blueprint areas deserve proportionate attention—but they should not crowd out the smaller areas that still contribute meaningful numbers of questions.

Smaller Blueprint Areas Still Matter

The remaining categories include:

  • orthodontics/pediatric/geriatric/special-needs dentistry;
  • periodontics;
  • oral surgery/trauma/orofacial pain/dental emergencies;
  • endodontics;
  • evidence-based dentistry/prevention/infection control/ethics/jurisprudence.

A common study mistake is to ignore a 5–8% category because it looks small.

With 200 questions, even a smaller blueprint area can materially affect the final result.

Current Terminology and Standards

NDEB states that examination questions are updated regularly as new knowledge becomes available.

That is one reason candidates should not rely exclusively on an old commercial question bank.

The current AFK page also notes that periodontics uses the 2017 American Academy of Periodontology disease classification.

For all NDEB examinations, candidates should also be comfortable with the FDI two-digit tooth-numbering system.

Computer-Based vs Booklet AFK

NDEB may deliver AFK in two formats.

Prometric computer-based examination

After electronic-exam registration is completed, NDEB provides instructions for scheduling the seat time.

Candidates registered at a Prometric centre are expected to review the NDEB AFK/ACJ electronic exam orientation.

Prometric also has its own check-in and security procedures.

Booklet/paper examination

Selected event sites can use a paper-and-pencil booklet format.

The content standard is the AFK; the delivery interface is what changes.

When registering, confirm the delivery method attached to the location you choose.

Scoring

After the examination, AFK Results 2027 explains the rescaled-75 standard, score reporting and verification process.

The current NDEB standard is:

  • 75 or greater: Pass
  • below 75: Fail

This is a test-equated, re-scaled score.

It is not the same as percentage correct.

NDEB also states that its examinations are not graded on a curve.

Therefore:

  • 75 does not mean exactly 75% correct;
  • 74 does not necessarily mean one question short;
  • another candidate's raw impression of the exam cannot tell you your likely scaled result.

For result interpretation, see AFK Results 2027.

Official Preparation Resources

Passing AFK moves you to the next stage, so keep ACJ Exam Dates 2027 and ACJ Registration 2027 in view when choosing an AFK sitting.

NDEB's own 2026 preparation guidance tells candidates to start with official materials.

AFK Protocol

Use it for:

  • blueprint;
  • format;
  • timing;
  • exam-day rules.

AFK Self-Assessment

The current web-based self-assessment contains 100 multiple-choice questions.

NDEB says it is designed to help candidates gauge readiness; the self-assessment result does not guarantee or prevent success on the actual AFK.

Released Question Bank

Use official released questions to understand the style and level of NDEB material.

Do not assume released questions are a complete prediction of the current examination.

Reference Text List

NDEB maintains a list of references used in Canadian dental education and also references journal articles, clinical guidelines and specialty-organization standards.

Electronic Exam Orientation

Prometric candidates should review the official orientation before exam day so interface mechanics do not become an unnecessary distraction.

A Practical Study Allocation

The complete sequence is organized in the NDEB Equivalency Timeline 2027 and the NDEB 2027 Exam Calendar.

The blueprint can guide study time without becoming a rigid percentage calendar.

A sensible approach is:

  1. give the largest blocks proportionally more study sessions;
  2. keep every blueprint category active throughout preparation;
  3. use mixed practice so biomedical and clinical knowledge remain integrated;
  4. periodically compare your study distribution with the official blueprint;
  5. use official released material to calibrate style rather than memorize recalled questions.

The exact amount of time you need depends on your current knowledge and weaknesses.

Common Mistakes

  • Studying from an unofficial “2027 syllabus” that NDEB never published.
  • Treating 75 as 75% correct.
  • Preparing only clinical dentistry and neglecting biomedical sciences.
  • Ignoring smaller blueprint areas.
  • Assuming the blueprint percentages are exact fixed question counts.
  • Using recalled or reconstructed exam questions.
  • Not practising the FDI numbering system.
  • Waiting until exam day to learn the Prometric interface.

FAQs

1. Is the AFK still 200 questions in 2027?

Under the latest published protocol, yes: 200 single-answer MCQs in two parts.

2. How much time do I get?

Two hours per part, four hours total examination time, with one scheduled break.

3. What is the official AFK syllabus?

NDEB uses the KSAs and the AFK blueprint rather than a chapter-by-chapter syllabus.

4. Is there negative marking?

No. The current protocol says there is no penalty for incorrect answers.

5. How many times can I take AFK?

A maximum of three attempts under current NDEB rules.
