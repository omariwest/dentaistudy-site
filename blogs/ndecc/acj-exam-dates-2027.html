ACJ Exam Dates 2027: May and November Sessions & Deadlines

The National Dental Examining Board of Canada (NDEB) administers the Assessment of Clinical Judgement (ACJ) twice per year—once in May and once in November—as the second examination in the Equivalency Process for graduates of non-accredited dental programs. Missing a registration opening timestamp or misunderstanding the NDEB's multi-window booking and regional transfer rules can easily delay your licensure timeline by six months or more.

This guide compiles the confirmed ACJ exam dates 2027 directly from official NDEB schedules, breaks down the three-step registration and Prometric scheduling workflow, explains the two-window registration policy that took effect on September 1, 2026, and separates official NDEB deadlines from practical adviser recommendations.

Quick Answers: 2027 ACJ Schedule at a Glance

  • When is the May 2027 ACJ exam? The confirmed May 2027 ACJ takes place on May 4, 2027 in Canada and the United States, and on May 5, 2027 in Australia and New Zealand.
  • When is the November 2027 ACJ exam? The confirmed November 2027 ACJ in Canada takes place on November 2, 2027. International and U.S. dates for November 2027 remain unconfirmed on the NDEB calendar as of November 17, 2026.
  • When does registration open for the 2027 ACJ sessions? First-window registration for the May 2027 ACJ opens on December 8, 2026 at 10:00 ET (Canada/U.S.) and December 15, 2026 at 10:00 ET (Australia/New Zealand). First-window registration for the November 2027 ACJ in Canada opens on June 8, 2027 at 10:00 ET.
  • What are the final registration deadlines? All registration activity closes at 21:00 ET on April 5, 2027 for the May 2027 sitting and 21:00 ET on October 4, 2027 for the November 2027 sitting.
  • How do the new two-window registration rules work? Under NDEB rules effective September 1, 2026, the first registration period closes as soon as locations reach capacity, and a dedicated second registration window opens roughly 6 to 8 weeks before the exam to release seats freed by withdrawals or added capacity.
  • How much does the ACJ cost and how many attempts are allowed? The published ACJ examination fee is $1,350 CAD (subject to annual NDEB review), and candidates are permitted a maximum of three attempts after passing the Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge (AFK).

Examination Locations & Delivery Format

The ACJ is a computer-based examination administered primarily through Prometric test centres, supplemented by NDEB-hosted testing sites (such as NDEB's Ottawa facility) when listed during registration.

Regional Structure & Delivery Differences

  1. North America (Canada & United States): For May 4, 2027, NDEB lists testing availability across both Canada and the United States. For November 2, 2027, confirmed availability is currently listed for Canada. When registering in NDEBConnect, candidates select a specific city or site option.
  2. Australia & New Zealand: For the May 2027 sitting, testing in Australia and New Zealand takes place on May 5, 2027 to align with the North American exam administration across time zones. Registration for these locations opens exactly seven days after North America (December 15, 2026 at 10:00 ET).
  3. Prometric vs. NDEB-Hosted Sites: If you register for a Prometric-delivered location, you must complete a separate seat-time booking step through Prometric after NDEB registration closes. If you register for an NDEB-hosted site, NDEB assigns your seating directly and you do not schedule a separate appointment on Prometric's portal.

Eligibility, Attempt Limits & How ACJ Differs from AFK and NDECC

Before planning around the 2027 calendar, verify that your Equivalency Process status meets NDEB's prerequisites and that you are preparing specifically for the ACJ blueprint rather than conflating it with adjacent exams.

Mandatory Eligibility Rules

  • Approved Equivalency Process Application: You must hold an active, approved Equivalency Process profile with the NDEB. Initial credential verification takes approximately 10 weeks when all academic documents are submitted accurately on the first attempt, and you cannot register for any exam until credential verification is approved.
  • Passing AFK Result: You become eligible to register for the ACJ only after passing the Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge (AFK).
  • Three-Attempt Lifetime Limit: You may attempt the ACJ a maximum of three times. An official withdrawal submitted before the start of the examination does not count as an attempt, whereas sitting the exam or failing to appear without withdrawing affects your standing and fees.
  • Passing Standard & Score Reporting: NDEB enforces a standardized passing score of 75 (test-equated, re-scaled). Passing candidates receive a "Pass" result with no numerical breakdown; failing candidates receive "Fail" along with their re-scaled score and have 30 days from results release to request a score verification ($200 CAD fee).

How NDEB Registration & Prometric Scheduling Work

Registering for the ACJ is not a single click—it is a three-step sequence split between NDEBConnect and Prometric.

Step 1: Register and Pay on NDEBConnect

Log in to your NDEBConnect profile at 10:00 ET on your region's registration opening date. Select your preferred examination location/city and submit the $1,350 CAD examination fee. Your registration is not confirmed until payment clears and your NDEBConnect dashboard reflects a registered status.

Step 2: Receive NDEB Scheduling Notification

After the registration deadline passes (after April 5, 2027 for the May exam, or after October 4, 2027 for the November exam), NDEB emails official scheduling instructions to registered Prometric candidates. Do not attempt to book your ACJ appointment directly on Prometric's website before receiving your NDEB scheduling notification and eligibility details.

Step 3: Schedule Your Seat Time on Prometric

Once notified by NDEB, visit Prometric.com, enter your eligibility details, and select your specific test-centre address and start time for the exam date. Print or save your Prometric confirmation notice, and review the NDEB ACJ Protocol and Electronic Exam Orientation video prior to exam day.

Official Deadlines vs. Adviser Recommendations

When organizing your calendar, separate hard NDEB rules from strategic best practices:

  • Official NDEB Deadlines (Strict Cutoffs):
  • Registration opening timestamps (10:00 ET on Dec 8, 2026; Dec 15, 2026; and June 8, 2027).
  • Final registration and standard withdrawal deadlines (21:00 ET on April 5, 2027 and October 4, 2027).
  • Score verification request window (30 days from results release).
  • Adviser Recommendations (Practical Safeguards):
  • Log in 15 minutes early on opening day: Because first-window registration closes as soon as seats fill, logging in at 09:45 ET with your payment method ready prevents login queue delays.
  • Schedule Prometric within 24 hours of Step 2 email: Although your NDEB registration guarantees a seat in your registered city/region, Prometric test centres within a metropolitan area fill early morning time slots first.
  • Budget ~8 weeks for results: NDEB generally releases ACJ results within eight weeks of the exam date, though exact release dates are not guaranteed in advance.

New Two-Window Registration Rules, Transfers & Cancellation Policies

In 2026, the NDEB introduced two major structural updates to how candidates book, transfer, and cancel Equivalency Process exams. Every 2027 ACJ candidate must plan around these rules:

1. The Two-Window Registration System (Effective September 1, 2026)

Historically, candidates whose preferred city sold out had to refresh NDEBConnect continuously for months hoping to catch a dropped seat. Effective September 1, 2026, NDEB replaced continuous seat hunting with two structured periods:

  • First Registration Period: Opens at 10:00 ET on the published opening date and closes immediately once all examination locations reach capacity.
  • Second Registration Period: Opens approximately 6 to 8 weeks prior to the examination date (for example, mid-to-late March 2027 for the May 4, 2027 ACJ, and mid-September 2027 for the November 2, 2027 ACJ—exact timestamps are announced by NDEB before opening). During this window, candidates can register for seats freed by earlier withdrawals or newly added capacity.

2. Regional Staggering & Inter-Regional Transfer Ban (Effective June 1, 2026)

  • One-Week North American Lead: Canadian and U.S. registration opens one week before Australia and New Zealand.
  • Strict Regional Lock-In: Candidates registered in Australia or New Zealand cannot transfer to Canada or the United States, and candidates registered in Canada or the United States cannot transfer to Australia or New Zealand. Choose your continent carefully before submitting Step 1 payment.

3. Transfer & Withdrawal Fee Schedule

  • When Transfers Are Allowed: You may transfer between eligible test locations (within the same region) only while a registration period is open (either during the First Registration Period before it fills, or during the Second Registration Period). Transfers are blocked between registration periods.
  • Withdrawal Fees (Effective September 1, 2026):
  • Before the official registration deadline (21:00 ET on April 5 or October 4, 2027): 50% withdrawal fee (you forfeit $675 CAD and receive $675 CAD back on a $1,350 CAD fee).
  • After the official registration deadline (or no-show on exam day): 100% withdrawal fee (no refund).
  • Approved Serious Medical or Unanticipated Exceptional Circumstance: 25% withdrawal fee (requires formal documentation and NDEB approval per NDEB withdrawal policy).

Practical Candidate Planning & Common Mistakes

Realistic Planning Scenarios for 2027 Candidates

  • Scenario A: You passed the August 2026 (or earlier) AFK.

You already hold an active AFK pass in NDEBConnect. Your primary target is December 8, 2026 at 10:00 ET (North America) or December 15, 2026 at 10:00 ET (Australia/New Zealand) to secure a seat in the First Registration Period for the May 2027 ACJ. This gives you roughly five months of focused clinical case and radiology preparation.

  • Scenario B: You are sitting the February 5–6, 2027 AFK.

Trying to jump straight from the February 2027 AFK to the May 4, 2027 ACJ is tight and carries seat-availability risk. Because AFK results generally take up to eight weeks, February 2027 AFK scores are expected around early April 2027—just days before the April 5, 2027 (21:00 ET) registration deadline for the May ACJ. By April, the First Registration Period will have closed, meaning your only chance for May 2027 would be remaining seats in the Second Registration Period (if still open when your AFK result posts). Treat November 2, 2027 (registration opening June 8, 2027 at 10:00 ET) as your primary, lower-risk target unless a late seat opens in April.

Common Candidate Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Confusing Eastern Time (ET) with Local Time: All NDEB opening (10:00 ET) and closing (21:00 ET) timestamps are strictly Eastern Time. A candidate in Vancouver (PT) who logs in at 10:00 AM local time is three hours late (13:00 ET)—long after first-window seats have filled.
  2. Booking Non-Refundable Travel Across Regions: Registering in Australia or New Zealand as a "backup" while living in North America locks you into that region, because NDEB prohibits inter-regional transfers.
  3. Treating ACJ Like a Single-Answer Recall Test: Unlike the AFK, the ACJ relies heavily on multi-question clinical cases, radiographic interpretation, and "Select ONE OR MORE correct answers" items where partial selections receive zero credit.
  4. Ignoring NDEBConnect After April 5 or October 4: Paying NDEB does not assign your Prometric exam centre. Candidates who miss the Step 2 notification email risk limited test-centre choices during Step 3 scheduling.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can I register for the May 2027 ACJ while waiting for my February 2027 AFK results?

No. NDEB eligibility rules require a confirmed passing result on the Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge (AFK) before NDEBConnect permits you to register for the ACJ. If you take the February 5, 2027 AFK, you must wait until your passing grade is posted before attempting to book a May 2027 ACJ seat.

2. What happens if all locations sell out on December 8, 2026 or June 8, 2027?

Under NDEB's two-window registration policy, the First Registration Period closes once all locations reach capacity. You do not need to refresh the portal daily; instead, monitor NDEB announcements for the Second Registration Period, which opens approximately 6 to 8 weeks before the exam to release seats freed by withdrawals or added capacity.

3. When are 2027 ACJ exam results released?

NDEB does not publish fixed result-release dates in advance, stating that results are typically issued within eight weeks of the examination. Based on an eight-week turnaround, May 4, 2027 ACJ candidates can generally anticipate results around late June 2027, and November 2, 2027 candidates around late December 2027—though candidates should wait for official NDEB notification before making firm NDECC plans.

4. If I withdraw from the 2027 ACJ, do I lose one of my three exam attempts?

No, provided you submit an official withdrawal through NDEBConnect before the examination begins. However, financial penalties still apply: withdrawing before the official registration deadline (21:00 ET on April 5 or October 4, 2027) incurs a 50% fee penalty, while withdrawing after the deadline forfeits 100% of the fee (unless approved under NDEB's 25% serious medical or exceptional circumstance policy).

5. Is the ACJ offered in French as well as English?

Yes. All NDEB Equivalency Process examinations and official preparation documents—including the ACJ Protocol and sample questions—are available in both English and French. You select your preferred examination language during registration.
