AFK Registration 2027: Booking, Seat Availability & Deadlines

Registering for the Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge (AFK) is a time-sensitive administrative task that sits between eligibility approval and examination day. For internationally trained dentists preparing for Canadian dental licensing, the registration process changed significantly in late 2026. The National Dental Examining Board of Canada (NDEB) introduced a second registration period, revised withdrawal fee structure, and region-specific opening dates for North America versus Australia and New Zealand .

Quick Answers

When does AFK registration open for February 2027? Registration opens on 15 September 2026 for North America and 22 September 2026 for Australia and New Zealand .

What is the second registration window? A second registration period opens approximately 6–8 weeks before the examination. Candidates may register or transfer only if seats become available through withdrawals or increased capacity .

Are additional seats announced? Yes, when NDEB increases capacity. The official dates page lists additional seats for the February 2027 AFK opening on 22 December 2026 at 10:00 ET . This is a specific announcement, not a recurring guarantee.

Do I schedule with Prometric immediately after NDEB registration? No. For electronic examinations, you must wait until after the NDEB registration deadline. Scheduling instructions then appear under “Documents” in your NDEBConnect registration .

Can I transfer between North America and Australia/New Zealand? No. Transfers between these regions are not permitted . Transfers between locations within the same region are possible only while registration is open .

This article focuses exclusively on the mechanics of securing an AFK seat for the 2027 sessions. It does not replace the official examination calendar. Instead, it explains what you must do inside NDEBConnect, how electronic and paper examinations diverge at the scheduling stage, and what realistic options exist when your preferred location shows no availability. All policy statements below are drawn from NDEB and Prometric publications. Practical advice is clearly separated and never guarantees a seat.

Before choosing a registration window, confirm the actual sitting on AFK Exam Dates 2027 and review AFK 2027 Format and Syllabus so your booking target matches your preparation timeline.

If you secure a seat, the next stage after the examination is covered in AFK Results 2027; a pass then opens the path to ACJ Registration 2027.

Eligibility Before You Touch the Registration Button

For the wider sequence beyond registration, use the NDEB Equivalency Timeline 2027 and the master 2027 calendar.

NDEBConnect will not display registration options for the AFK unless your Equivalency Process application has been approved. There is no provisional or conditional registration pathway. The NDEB explicitly states that only candidates with approved applications are eligible to register .

Two timing risks deserve attention. First, application review times vary. Submitting your equivalency application only days before registration opens creates a real possibility that approval will not arrive in time to join the first registration period. Second, the AFK registration queue is separate from the application queue. An approved application is a prerequisite, not a placeholder for a seat.

Once approved, your NDEBConnect account becomes the sole channel for registration, payment, document access, and transfer requests. Do not expect to register by email or phone.

Understanding the Two Registration Periods

The August 2026 announcement created a structural change that every 2027 candidate should understand before registration day .

Registration PeriodTimingWhat It AllowsWhat It Does Not Allow
First registrationOpens on a set date and time; closes when all locations are fullNew registrations and paymentsTransfers after closure
Second registrationOpens about 6–8 weeks before the examRegistration or transfer if seats become available through withdrawals or increased capacityGuaranteed seats; transfers between periods

The practical consequence is important. The first period is not a deadline-driven window that remains open until the published registration deadline. It closes when capacity is exhausted, which can happen well before the deadline. The second period is a release valve, not a second chance for everyone. It exists because candidates withdraw and because NDEB occasionally adds capacity. It does not create new seats out of nothing.

A separate regional rule applies from June 2026 onward. North American locations open registration one week before Australia and New Zealand locations. Transfers between these two regions are prohibited, regardless of registration period .

Step-by-Step Registration in NDEBConnect

Step 1: Log in and confirm your eligibility status

Access your NDEBConnect account before registration opens. Confirm that your Equivalency Process application shows as approved. If it does not, registration options will not appear, and no amount of refreshing will change that.

Step 2: Enter the registration module at the published opening time

Registration opens at a specific hour, not merely a date. For the February 2027 AFK, North America opens 15 September 2026 and Australia/New Zealand opens 22 September 2026 . Log in shortly before the stated time. The NDEB has previously advised candidates to prepare for registration day and has provided a “Being Registration Ready” video in its library .

Step 3: Select an available location and session

Only locations with space available will be displayed . You cannot place a hold on a seat. You cannot select a location that shows full and expect a waitlist. If the location you want is not listed, it is not available at that moment.

Step 4: Choose paper or electronic delivery

The AFK is offered in two formats: a paper-and-pencil examination at event sites and a computer-based examination at Prometric test centres in Canada and select international locations . Your format choice affects the next steps.

  • Paper examination: After registration and payment, no additional scheduling step is required . Your registration confirmation contains the venue details.
  • Electronic examination: You must schedule your seat time with Prometric after the NDEB registration deadline. Instructions will be posted under “Documents” in your registration .

Step 5: Pay the registration fee

Payment completes the registration. NDEBConnect processes the fee as part of the registration flow. Do not treat a selected location as secure until payment is confirmed.

Step 6: Monitor your registration documents

For electronic candidates, the Prometric scheduling window opens after the NDEB deadline. The exact instructions and eligibility number needed for Prometric appear in your NDEBConnect registration documents. Prometric cannot schedule you before NDEB has completed this handoff .

Paper vs. Electronic: Where the Registration Paths Diverge

The registration and payment steps in NDEBConnect are identical for both formats. The divergence occurs immediately afterward.

StagePaper AFKElectronic AFK (Prometric)
NDEBConnect registrationRequiredRequired
PaymentRequiredRequired
Prometric schedulingNot applicableRequired after NDEB deadline
Scheduling instructionsVenue details in registrationPosted under “Documents” in registration
Seat selectionAssigned by NDEB event siteSelected through Prometric
Orientation requirementNone specific to deliveryAFK and ACJ Electronic Exam Orientation must be viewed before testing

The Prometric handoff is the most common source of confusion. Candidates sometimes attempt to search for the NDEB on the Prometric website immediately after paying NDEB. Prometric’s own test-owner page confirms the sequence: you must register and pay through NDEB first, and only then can you schedule . Prometric does not have a self-service path for candidates who have not received the NDEB scheduling instructions.

The AFK protocol also states that if you are registered at a Prometric test centre, the NDEB’s AFK and ACJ Electronic Exam Orientation must be viewed prior to taking the AFK . Treat this as a registration-adjacent requirement, not an optional study resource.

Seat Availability, Full Locations, and the Second Window

What “full” actually means

NDEB registration is capacity-based. When the available seats at a location are allocated, the location disappears from the selection screen. The NDEB states plainly that examinations fill up prior to the registration deadline, and that being unable to register is not necessarily a sign that something is wrong with your account .

What to do when your preferred location is full

  1. Check the official dates page for a specific additional-seat announcement. For February 2027, NDEB has published that additional seats will open on 22 December 2026 at 10:00 ET . This is a concrete, dated opportunity. It is not a guarantee that a seat will be available for you.
  2. Wait for the second registration period. It opens roughly 6–8 weeks before the examination and permits registration or transfer if seats become available through withdrawals or increased capacity .
  3. Consider a different location within your region. Transfers are permitted only while registration is open, and only within the same regional framework .
  4. Do not attempt to register in Australia/New Zealand as a backdoor if you intend to test in North America. Transfers between these regions are prohibited, and the registration dates are deliberately staggered .

What not to do

Do not refresh the registration page continuously for hours or days. The second registration window was designed specifically to remove the need for constant monitoring . Use the published dates. If a specific additional-seat release has been announced, be ready at that time. Otherwise, wait for the second window.

Do not assume that a full first period means you will automatically receive a seat in the second period. The second period depends on withdrawals and capacity changes. Neither is guaranteed.

Transfers and Withdrawals

Transfers

Transfers are permitted only when registration is open. They are not permitted between registration periods . This means that if you registered in the first period and later want to change locations, you must do so before the first period closes. Once the first period closes, your next transfer opportunity falls within the second registration period, and only if seats are available at your desired location.

There is no transfer between North America and Australia/New Zealand under any circumstances .

Withdrawals

The withdrawal fee structure changed effective 1 September 2026 and applies to future AFK, ACJ, and Virtual OSCE examinations :

Timing of WithdrawalFee
Before the registration deadline50% of the examination fee
After the registration deadline100% of the examination fee
Approved medical or exceptional-circumstance withdrawalUnchanged (separate policy)

The financial consequence of withdrawing after the deadline is severe. If you are uncertain about your ability to sit the examination, resolve that uncertainty before the registration deadline, not after.

Common Booking Mistakes

Mistake 1: Registering without an approved application. NDEBConnect will not offer the AFK registration option. There is no workaround.

Mistake 2: Treating the registration deadline as the closing time. The first registration period closes when locations are full, which can be weeks before the published deadline .

Mistake 3: Trying to schedule with Prometric before receiving NDEB instructions. Prometric requires the NDEB registration to be complete and the scheduling window to be open. Early attempts waste time and create confusion .

Mistake 4: Registering in the wrong region. Candidates who register in Australia/New Zealand and later want to transfer to Canada or the United States cannot do so .

Mistake 5: Missing the additional-seat announcement. Specific seat releases, such as the 22 December 2026 release for February 2027, are published on the dates page. They are not emailed individually as a guarantee .

Mistake 6: Assuming the second window is a guaranteed second chance. It is contingent on withdrawals and capacity. Candidates have lost entire examination cycles by relying on the second window without a backup plan .

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I register for the AFK before my Equivalency Process application is approved? No. Only candidates with approved applications can register. The registration option will not appear in NDEBConnect until approval is recorded .

I registered for the electronic AFK. When can I choose my Prometric test date and time? After the NDEB registration deadline. Scheduling instructions will be posted under “Documents” in your NDEBConnect registration. You cannot schedule before that handoff .

The February 2027 first registration period is full. Should I wait for the second window? The second window is a legitimate opportunity, but it is not a guarantee. Check the official dates page for any announced additional-seat release, such as the 22 December 2026 opening , and prepare to act at that time if it applies to your location.

Can I transfer from a Canadian Prometric site to an Australian site after registering? No. Transfers between North America and Australia/New Zealand are prohibited . Transfers within a region are possible only while registration is open .

What happens if I withdraw after the registration deadline? You forfeit 100% of the examination fee, unless the withdrawal is approved under the medical or exceptional-circumstance policy .
