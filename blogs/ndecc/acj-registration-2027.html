ACJ Registration 2027: Prometric Booking, Deadlines & Transfers

Securing a seat for the Assessment of Clinical Judgement (ACJ) requires far more than simply logging into your portal before a final closing date. Under National Dental Examining Board of Canada (NDEB) registration policies—including regional staggered openings effective June 1, 2026 and the two-registration-period structure effective September 1, 2026—the first registration period closes when all exam locations are full, transfers are restricted to active registration windows, and late withdrawals carry steep financial penalties.

Quick Answers: 2027 ACJ Registration Rules & Dates

  • Who is eligible to register for the 2027 ACJ? You become eligible to register in NDEBConnect only after receiving a passing result on the Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge (AFK) and provided you have not exhausted your maximum of three ACJ attempts.
  • When does May 2027 ACJ registration open? First-window registration opens on December 8, 2026 at 10:00 ET for Canada and the United States (exam date May 4, 2027), and one week later on December 15, 2026 at 10:00 ET for Australia and New Zealand (exam date May 5, 2027).
  • When does November 2027 ACJ registration open? For Canada (exam date November 2, 2027), first-window registration opens on June 8, 2027 at 10:00 ET. November 2027 registration dates for the United States, Australia, and New Zealand are currently unannounced.
  • What are the 2027 registration deadlines? Registration closes at 21:00 ET on April 5, 2027 for the May 2027 sittings and at 21:00 ET on October 4, 2027 for the November 2027 sitting.
  • How do the two registration periods work? The First Registration Period closes immediately once all exam locations reach capacity; a Second Registration Period opens roughly 6 to 8 weeks before the exam to release seats freed by withdrawals or expanded capacity, though seat availability in the second window is never guaranteed.
  • Can I transfer my ACJ location after registering? You may transfer between locations within the same region only while a registration period is open—transfers are prohibited between registration periods and between North America and Australia/New Zealand.

Eligibility Requirements Before You Register

If you are still at the earlier stage, AFK Results 2027 explains when a pass unlocks ACJ eligibility. For the full sequence, see the NDEB Equivalency Timeline 2027.

NDEBConnect controls exam access automatically based on your Equivalency Process standing:

  1. Confirmed AFK Pass: You must hold a verified passing grade on the Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge (AFK) inside your NDEBConnect profile before the ACJ registration button becomes active. Candidates still awaiting AFK results cannot pre-book or hold an ACJ seat. (If you are still completing the AFK stage, review our AFK Exam Dates 2027 and AFK Registration 2027 guides.)
  2. Three-Attempt Lifetime Limit: NDEB permits a maximum of three attempts at the ACJ. If you have already recorded three unsuccessful ACJ attempts, you are ineligible to register again.
  3. Active Profile & No Unresolved Administrative Holds: Ensure your NDEBConnect profile has no outstanding document verifications or unpaid administrative fees before opening day.

Step-by-Step ACJ Registration & Prometric Scheduling Process

Registering for the ACJ is a three-step process split between your NDEBConnect portal and Prometric's scheduling platform. Paying NDEB in Step 1 reserves your eligibility for your chosen region/city, not your specific Prometric test-centre address.

Step 1: Register and Pay on NDEBConnect

  • Log in to NDEBConnect right at 10:00 ET on your region's registration opening date (December 8, 2026 for Canada/U.S.; December 15, 2026 for Australia/New Zealand; June 8, 2027 for November Canada).
  • Select your preferred examination location/city from the active dropdown list.
  • Pay the $1,350 CAD ACJ examination fee online. Your registration is only valid once payment processes and your NDEBConnect dashboard displays a confirmed registration status.

Step 2: Receive Your NDEB Scheduling Notification

  • After the registration window closes (or as NDEB processes candidate rosters following the registration deadline), NDEB sends an official scheduling notification email to candidates registered for Prometric-delivered locations.
  • Do not attempt to book an ACJ appointment directly on Prometric's website before receiving your NDEB notification email and eligibility details.

Step 3: Schedule Your Seat Time on Prometric

  • Once your NDEB scheduling notification arrives, go to Prometric.com, enter your NDEB eligibility credentials, and select your specific test-centre street address and start time for May 4, 2027 (North America), May 5, 2027 (Australia/New Zealand), or November 2, 2027 (Canada).
  • Download and save your Prometric appointment confirmation immediately, and verify that your registered name matches your government-issued ID character-for-character.

The Two-Registration-Period Policy: What Happens When Locations Fill

Effective September 1, 2026, the NDEB overhauled how seats are released for the AFK, ACJ, and Virtual OSCE to eliminate the need for candidates to refresh NDEBConnect continuously throughout the day once venues fill.

How the Two Windows Operate

  1. First Registration Period: Opens on the published opening date at 10:00 ET and closes immediately once all examination locations reach capacity. Even though the final registration deadline is months later (April 5 or October 4, 2027), the first registration period closes as soon as all listed exam locations are full.
  2. Closed Interim Period: Once all locations fill, registration remains closed. Cancelled seats are not dripped back into the portal at random hours.
  3. Second Registration Period: Opens approximately 6 to 8 weeks before the examination date (for example, mid-to-late March 2027 for the May 4, 2027 ACJ, and mid-September 2027 for the November 2, 2027 ACJ—exact timestamps are posted on NDEB's website before opening). During this second window, candidates can register for seats freed by earlier withdrawals or newly added test-centre capacity.

No Guarantee of Second-Window Availability

If you miss the First Registration Period, a seat in the Second Registration Period is never guaranteed. Second-window inventory depends entirely on how many registered candidates withdraw or whether NDEB secures extra workstations, and available seats are claimed rapidly on a first-come, first-served basis. Always treat the First Registration Period opening timestamp as your primary registration target.

Location Transfers & Regional Lock-In Rules

Two strict NDEB policies govern whether you can change your examination location after completing Step 1:

1. Transfers Allowed Only While Registration Is Open

  • Under the September 1, 2026 registration rules, candidates may transfer between available test locations only while a registration period is open (either during the First Registration Period before locations fill, or during the Second Registration Period if seats open).
  • Prohibition Between Registration Periods: You cannot transfer between test centres during the closed gap between the First Registration Period and Second Registration Period.

2. Strict Ban on Inter-Regional Transfers

  • Effective June 1, 2026, NDEB opens North American locations one week ahead of Australia and New Zealand and enforces a strict regional lock-in:
  • Candidates registered in Canada or the United States cannot transfer to Australia or New Zealand.
  • Candidates registered in Australia or New Zealand cannot transfer to Canada or the United States.
  • Do not register in an overseas region as a temporary placeholder—you will be locked into travelling to that region or paying a withdrawal penalty.

Practical Candidate Planning & Common Registration Mistakes

Pre-Registration Checklist (48 Hours Before Opening Day)

  • Verify NDEBConnect Login & Payment Card: Log in to NDEBConnect two days before opening day to confirm your password works, your AFK pass is recorded, and your credit card is cleared for a $1,350 CAD charge without international or fraud blocks.
  • Convert 10:00 ET to Your Local Timezone:
  • Vancouver / Pacific Time (PT): 07:00 AM
  • Calgary / Mountain Time (MT): 08:00 AM
  • Toronto / Ottawa / Eastern Time (ET): 10:00 AM
  • Sydney / Melbourne (AEDT on Dec 15, 2026): 02:00 AM the next day (Dec 16)
  • Rank Your Top 3 Cities Within Your Region: Because you cannot transfer between North America and Australia/New Zealand, decide in advance which domestic or regional cities you are genuinely willing to travel to if your first-choice city sells out within minutes.

Planning Around February 2027 AFK Results

Candidates sitting the February 5–6, 2027 AFK face a narrow window if they hope to take the May 4, 2027 ACJ:

  • AFK results generally take up to eight weeks, placing February 2027 AFK result release around late March or early April 2027—just days before the April 5, 2027 (21:00 ET) registration deadline for the May ACJ.
  • Because the First Registration Period for May 2027 will have closed months earlier, February 2027 AFK passers can only enter the May 2027 ACJ if the Second Registration Period is still open and has remaining seats when their AFK grade posts. Treat November 2, 2027 (registration opening June 8, 2027 at 10:00 ET) as your primary, dependable target.

Common Registration Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Logging In at 10:00 AM Local Time Instead of 10:00 ET: Every year, candidates in Western Canada or overseas miss first-window seats because they miscalculate Eastern Time.
  2. Thinking Step 1 Payment Books Your Prometric Address: Completing Step 1 in NDEBConnect only registers you for your region/city—you must watch for your Step 2 NDEB email and complete Step 3 on Prometric.com to lock in your actual testing centre and start time.
  3. Booking Non-Refundable Travel for a Second-Window Gamble: Never buy non-refundable flights or hotels for May 4 or November 2, 2027 unless you hold a confirmed Step 1 NDEBConnect registration.
  4. Waiting Until After April 5 or October 4 to Withdraw: If you are unwell or unprepared, withdrawing before 21:00 ET on the registration deadline saves 50% ($675 CAD) of your fee compared to waiting one day past the deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can I register for the May 2027 ACJ before my AFK results are released?

No. NDEB eligibility rules strictly require a confirmed passing result on the AFK inside your NDEBConnect profile before the system allows you to register for the ACJ.

2. When does the Second Registration Period open for the 2027 ACJ exams?

The umbrella NDEB 2027 Exam Calendar helps compare this ACJ window with AFK and NDECC timing before you commit to travel.

Under NDEB's two-registration-period policy effective September 1, 2026, the Second Registration Period opens approximately 6 to 8 weeks before the examination date. NDEB announces the exact opening date and timestamp on its website ahead of the window, though seat availability is never guaranteed.

3. Can I transfer my ACJ registration from Canada to Australia (or vice versa)?

No. Effective June 1, 2026, NDEB prohibits transfers between Canada/United States locations and Australia/New Zealand locations. Intra-regional location transfers are permitted only while a registration period is actively open.

4. How much is the ACJ registration fee in 2027, and what is the refund if I withdraw?

The ACJ examination fee is $1,350 CAD. If you withdraw via NDEBConnect before the official registration deadline (21:00 ET on April 5, 2027 for May or October 4, 2027 for November), NDEB charges a 50% withdrawal fee ($675 CAD refund). Withdrawing after the registration deadline forfeits 100% of the fee, except for approved serious medical or unanticipated exceptional circumstances (25% withdrawal fee).

5. Why are November 2027 ACJ registration dates only listed for Canada right now?

On NDEB's Examination Dates and Locations schedule as of November 17, 2026, only Canada (exam date November 2, 2027, registration opening June 8, 2027 at 10:00 ET) is confirmed. Whether U.S., Australian, or New Zealand locations will be added for November 2027 has not yet been announced by NDEB.
