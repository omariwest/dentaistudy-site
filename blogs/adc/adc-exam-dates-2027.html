ADC Exam Dates 2027: Written, Practical & Application Calendar

For internationally qualified dentists pursuing general dental registration in Australia, the Australian Dental Council (ADC) assessment calendar dictates every major milestone of the year. Unlike self-scheduled licensing tests that can be booked any week of the year, ADC examinations operate on fixed annual sittings with narrow application windows, strict initial-assessment cutoffs, and multi-month lead times between applying, sitting, and receiving results.

This guide brings together the confirmed ADC exam dates 2027 across both written examination sittings and all three practical examination periods. It separates verified official ADC dates from DentAIstudy planning advice, explains which dates are fixed versus broad testing windows, and shows how to sequence your 2027 applications without losing a six-month cycle.

Quick Answers: 2027 ADC Exam Calendar at a Glance

  • When are the 2027 ADC Written Examinations held? The ADC holds two written exam sittings in 2027: Wednesday 17 and Thursday 18 March 2027 (Period 1), and Wednesday 8 and Thursday 9 September 2027 (Period 2).
  • When do 2027 Written Exam applications open? Applications via ADC Connect open 8 – 15 December 2026 for the March 2027 exam and 8 – 15 June 2027 for the September 2027 exam.
  • What are the initial-assessment deadlines for 2027 Written Exams? Complete initial assessment applications must be submitted by 11 October 2026 for the March 2027 sitting and 11 April 2027 for the September 2027 sitting.
  • When are the 2027 ADC Practical Examinations held? Practical examinations run across three broad periods in Melbourne: January to May 2027 (Period 1), May – August 2027 (Period 2), and August to December 2027 (Period 3), with exact two-day dates allocated after each application window.
  • Where are the 2027 ADC exams held? The written examination is delivered via computer at authorised Pearson VUE test centres in Australia and internationally, whereas all practical examinations take place in person at the ADC Examination Centre in Melbourne, Victoria.
  • How much do ADC assessments cost in 2027? Following the ADC's 12-month fee freeze announced in June 2026, published fees are $647 AUD for initial assessment ($35 AUD renewal), $2,122 AUD for the written examination, and $4,775 AUD for the practical examination.

Exact Dates vs. Broader Examination Periods: How to Read the ADC Schedule

A frequent source of confusion for first-time candidates is that the ADC uses exact fixed dates for written examinations but multi-month examination periods for practical examinations. Understanding why these two formats differ is essential before booking international flights or requesting clinical leave.

Why Written Exam Dates Are Fixed Globally

The written examination is a computer-based test delivered simultaneously across Pearson VUE centres worldwide. Every candidate sitting in a given period completes the exact same four-section, 280-question examination across two consecutive weekdays (Wednesday and Thursday). Consequently, 17–18 March 2027 and 8–9 September 2027 are fixed global dates.

Why Practical Exam Dates Are Published as Multi-Month Ranges

Because every dentist practical examination takes place in person inside the ADC's clinical simulation and OSCE suites in Melbourne, cohort capacity per day is strictly capped. Rather than testing all candidates on the same two days, the ADC groups practical assessments into three overlapping operational windows—January to May 2027, May to August 2027, and August to December 2027.

Within each period:

  1. Candidates first apply for a place during the application period.
  2. Forty-eight hours after the application period closes, the ADC opens a date selection period (27–29 October 2026 for Period 1; 23–25 February 2027 for Period 2; 29 June–1 July 2027 for Period 3). Note that the date selection period closes early if all available examination dates are claimed before the end date.
  3. Official confirmation of your allocated two-day examination slot is uploaded to ADC Connect within six weeks of the close of the application period.

What ADC Has Not Yet Published for 2027: While the overarching application and date-selection windows for 2027 Periods 2 and 3 are confirmed, the ADC has not yet published whether 2027 Period 2 (10–19 February 2027) or Period 3 (16–25 June 2027) will use a two-stage priority split like Period 1 did in October 2026. Do not assume stage dates until the ADC releases a formal candidate news notice ahead of each window.

Stage 1: Initial Assessment Deadlines & Eligibility Rules

You cannot apply for a 2027 written examination unless you hold a successful, valid initial assessment whose expiry date extends beyond your chosen written examination date. For full documentation checklists, see our ADC Initial Assessment & Eligibility Guide.

Key Official Initial Assessment Rules

  • Standard Processing Timeframe: The ADC states an approximate processing timeframe of 8 weeks from receipt of a complete initial assessment application. Incomplete submissions or pending primary-source verifications extend this timeline.
  • Hard Submission Cutoffs for 2027 Written Exams:
  • To be considered for the March 2027 written exam, your complete initial assessment application had to be submitted by 11 October 2026 and assessed as eligible before the written application window closes on 15 December 2026.
  • To be considered for the September 2027 written exam, you must submit your complete initial assessment application on or before 11 April 2027, and it must be approved before the written application window closes on 15 June 2027.
  • No Guarantee Clause: Submitting by the initial assessment deadline does not guarantee written-exam eligibility if your file requires additional documents or clarification.
  • Seven-Year Validity & Renewal: Approved initial assessments remain valid for 7 years. If your initial assessment will expire before your 2027 written or practical exam date, you must submit a renewal application ($35 AUD) via ADC Connect before the expiry date. Allowing an initial assessment to lapse voids your eligibility and cancels any previously passed written examination results, forcing you to restart the entire process.

The Two-Step Written Exam Booking Sequence

  1. Apply via ADC Connect: At the opening of the 8-day application period (8 December 2026 or 8 June 2027), eligible candidates receive an automated email prompt. Log in to ADC Connect, verify that your passport and photograph identification are current, submit your application, and pay the $2,122 AUD examination fee before the window closes.
  2. Book Your Test Centre on Pearson VUE: In the weeks following a successful ADC application, Pearson VUE issues an Authorisation to Test (ATT) email opening a venue-booking window of approximately two weeks.
  • Same-Venue Rule: You must book the exact same Pearson VUE test centre for both examination days (Day 1 and Day 2).
  • Capacity Limits: Pearson VUE venues operate with strict seat caps allocated on a first-booked basis. Candidates outside Australia—or in high-demand Australian cities—may need to travel interstate or internationally if local seats fill early.

Validity, Attempts & Results Timeline

  • Five-Year Validity: A passing score in the written examination remains valid for 5 years from the date of result notification.
  • Unlimited Attempts: There is no cap on how many times you may sit the written examination, provided your initial assessment remains valid.
  • Results Release: The ADC estimates approximately 5 months from the start of the application period to results release—meaning March 2027 candidates typically receive results around May 2027, and September 2027 candidates around November 2027.

Stage 3: Progression to the 2027 Practical Examination

Passing the written examination unlocks eligibility to apply for the practical examination, the final stage of the ADC general dentistry assessment pathway. For station-by-station preparation strategies, review our ADC Practical Exam OSCE & Simulation Guide and our dedicated ADC Practical Exam Dates 2027 breakdown.

Practical Examination Eligibility Rules

To submit an application during any 2027 practical exam window, you must meet all three official criteria:

  1. Hold a valid initial assessment whose expiry date extends past the end of your practical examination date.
  2. Hold a valid written examination pass whose five-year validity extends past the end of your practical examination date.
  3. Not have sat, or be scheduled to sit, a practical examination in the immediately preceding examination period.

Where & How the Practical Exam Is Conducted

  • Single Global Location: Every ADC practical examination is held at the ADC Examination Centre, Level 6, 469 La Trobe Street, Melbourne, VIC 3000, Australia. There are no offshore practical test centres.
  • Two-Day Structure: Candidates attend across two consecutive weekdays (scheduled between 8:00 am and 5:00 pm): one Technical Skills Day (six restorative/prosthodontic/endodontic simulation tasks on typodont manikins) and one Clinical Skills Day (OSCE stations assessing communication, history-taking, diagnosis, and clinical reasoning). Candidates must be prepared to sit either component on Day 1.
  • Priority Staging & Waitlists: For 2027 Period 1 (October 2026 applications), the ADC divided the application period into Stage 1 (candidates whose initial assessment or written exam pass was expiring during or before the examination period) and Stage 2 (all other eligible candidates), followed by an electronic waitlist once applications closed. Expect similar priority mechanics if demand exceeds capacity in Periods 2 and 3, though exact stage dates are only confirmed shortly before each window opens.

Common 2027 Planning Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Treating the 11 April 2027 Deadline as a Guaranteed Entry Ticket: Submitting an initial assessment on 11 April 2027 only puts you in the queue for the September 2027 written exam. If your university or dental council delays verifying a document, your assessment will not finish before 15 June 2027, pushing your written exam to March 2028.
  2. Expecting to Sit the Practical Exam Right After the March 2027 Written Exam: Because 2027 Period 2 Practical applications close on 19 February 2027—nearly a month before the 17–18 March 2027 written exam—March written candidates cannot enter Period 2 Practical. Your earliest possible practical window is 2027 Period 3 (applying 16–25 June 2027).
  3. Booking Different Pearson VUE Centres for Day 1 and Day 2: Pearson VUE's booking portal allows you to view multiple test centres, which traps some candidates into booking Day 1 at one venue and Day 2 at another. ADC rules strictly require the same venue for both consecutive days.
  4. Letting a Seven-Year Initial Assessment Expire Mid-Pathway: Candidates who passed their written exam years earlier often focus solely on the five-year written validity and overlook their seven-year initial assessment expiry date. If your initial assessment expires before your practical exam date, you become ineligible unless you renew it before expiry.
  5. Missing the 48-Hour Date Selection Opening: Submitting a practical exam application does not automatically assign a date. You must return to ADC Connect 48 hours after the application window closes to claim a slot during the 3-day date selection period before preferred dates fill.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can I sit the ADC written exam in March 2027 and the practical exam in May 2027?

No. Applications for the 2027 Period 2 Practical Examination (held May – August 2027) run from 10 – 19 February 2027, which is before the 17 – 18 March 2027 written examination takes place. Because you must already hold a passing written result to apply for a practical place, March 2027 written candidates can first apply during the 16 – 25 June 2027 window for 2027 Period 3 (August – December 2027).

2. How long does it take to get ADC written exam results in 2027?

The ADC states an approximate timeframe of 5 months from the written examination application period to results release. In practice, this means results are generally released roughly 6 to 8 weeks after the examination dates (around May 2027 for the March sitting and November 2027 for the September sitting), in time for the next practical application window.

3. Can I take the 2027 ADC written exam outside Australia?

Yes. The ADC delivers the written examination in partnership with Pearson VUE at authorised test centres both inside Australia and internationally. However, test centres operate with capacity limits; if centres in your country fill during the ATT booking window, you may need to travel to another country to sit the exam.

4. Are there any offshore locations for the 2027 ADC practical examination?

No. All ADC practical examinations for dentists are conducted exclusively at the ADC Examination Centre at Level 6, 469 La Trobe Street, Melbourne, VIC 3000, Australia. International candidates must arrange their own Australian visa, flights, and accommodation for the two-day examination.

5. What happens if I fail the 2027 written or practical examination?

The ADC explicitly states that there is no limit on written examination attempts. For the practical examination, a candidate who does not pass may apply for another practical examination provided both the initial assessment and written examination remain valid. Current ADC guidance reviewed for this article does not state a separate numerical cap on practical attempts.
