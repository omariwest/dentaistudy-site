ADC Practical Exam Dates 2027: Periods 1–3 & Date Selection

The practical examination is the final assessment stage for internationally qualified dentists pursuing general dental registration in Australia. Unlike the computer-based written exam—which runs on two fixed global dates per year—the Australian Dental Council (ADC) organizes ADC practical exam dates 2027 into three multi-month operational periods, each governed by a strict 10-day application window followed 48 hours later by a 3-day date-selection window.

Quick Answers: 2027 ADC Practical Examination Schedule

  • When are the 2027 ADC practical examination periods? The ADC runs three general dentistry practical periods in 2027: Period 1 (January – May 2027), Period 2 (May – August 2027), and Period 3 (August – December 2027).
  • When do 2027 practical exam applications open? Period 1 applications ran 14 – 23 October 2026 (closed); Period 2 applications open 10 – 19 February 2027; and Period 3 applications open 16 – 25 June 2027.
  • How does date selection work? Exactly 48 hours after each application period closes, a three-day date-selection window opens in ADC Connect (27 – 29 October 2026 for Period 1; 23 – 25 February 2027 for Period 2; 29 June – 1 July 2027 for Period 3). The window closes early if all available examination dates are claimed before the end date.
  • Where are the 2027 practical exams held? All practical examinations take place in person at the ADC Examination Centre, Level 6, 469 La Trobe Street, Melbourne, VIC 3000, Australia.
  • How much is the 2027 practical exam fee? Following the ADC's 12-month fee freeze announced in June 2026, the published practical examination fee is $4,775 AUD.
  • How long do practical exam results take? The ADC estimates approximately 4 to 7 months from the start of the application period to results release.

Why Broad Examination Periods Are Not Your Exact Exam Date

A common misconception among first-time candidates is assuming that an examination window such as May – August 2027 means testing occurs on a single universal date or that you can walk in any week during those four months.

In reality, the ADC practical examination is a two-day in-person assessment consisting of:

  • One Technical Skills Day (simulated restorative, prosthodontic, and endodontic procedures on dental manikins—noting that the ADC removed the amalgam placement task starting from Period 2, 2026).
  • One Clinical Skills Day (Objective Structured Clinical Examination [OSCE] stations testing history-taking, diagnosis, communication, and clinical reasoning).

For full station details and clinical preparation strategies, review our ADC Practical Exam OSCE & Simulation Guide.

Because every candidate requires a dedicated simulation bay and calibrated examiners inside the ADC's Melbourne facility, the ADC groups individual two-day exam cohorts across a four-to-five-month operational window. Your personal attendance is limited to the specific two consecutive weekdays you select during the date-selection window and receive official confirmation for afterward.

Eligibility Rules & Validity Prerequisites

Candidates still working through earlier stages should use the ADC 2027 master calendar and ADC Initial Assessment 2027 rather than assuming practical eligibility follows automatically from a written pass.

Eligibility to apply for a 2027 practical examination is strictly verified inside ADC Connect. If your profile does not satisfy every criterion when an application window opens, you will not receive an invitation link to apply.

1. Valid Initial Assessment Through the Exam Date

You must hold a valid initial assessment whose seven-year expiry date falls later than your practical examination date. If your initial assessment is approaching its seven-year expiry during 2027, you must submit a renewal application ($35 AUD) via ADC Connect before the expiry date passes. Allowing an initial assessment to lapse cancels your eligibility and voids your written examination pass.

2. Valid Written Examination Pass Through the Exam Date

You must hold a successful, valid written examination result whose five-year expiry date also falls later than your practical examination date. Candidates awaiting written examination results cannot submit a placeholder application for a practical period.

3. Restriction on Consecutive Practical Periods

ADC eligibility rules prohibit candidates from applying for a practical examination period if they have already sat, or are currently scheduled to sit, a practical examination in the immediately preceding examination period. For example:

  • Candidates who sat—or held a confirmed booking—in 2026 Period 3 (August – December 2026) were excluded from applying during the 14 – 23 October 2026 window for 2027 Period 1.
  • Similarly, candidates sitting in 2027 Period 1 (January – May 2027) cannot apply during 10 – 19 February 2027 for 2027 Period 2; if they require a re-sit, their next eligible window is 2027 Period 3 (applying 16 – 25 June 2027 after receiving Period 1 results).

4. Period-Specific Advertised Criteria

In addition to baseline validity rules, candidates must satisfy any specific eligibility terms advertised by the ADC for that period (such as priority-stage rules if implemented).

How the Application, Date Selection & Confirmation Process Works

Securing a practical examination date in 2027 involves a four-step sequence managed through ADC Connect.

Step 1: Apply and Pay ($4,775 AUD) During the Application Period

At the start of the 10-day application period, all eligible candidates receive an automated email containing a direct link to apply via ADC Connect. You must complete the application form, verify that your personal details and photographic ID are current, and pay the $4,775 AUD practical examination fee before the window closes. Once payment is processed, the ADC emails an application receipt and updates your ADC Connect dashboard.

Step 2: Select Your Preferred Two-Day Session (Opens 48 Hours Later)

Applying and paying in Step 1 secures your place in the cohort, not your specific calendar dates. Exactly 48 hours after the application period closes, the ADC opens the three-day date selection period:

  • Period 2 Date Selection: 23 – 25 February 2027
  • Period 3 Date Selection: 29 June – 1 July 2027

During this window, you log in to ADC Connect and choose from available two-day sessions. Crucial rule: The date selection period closes early as soon as all examination dates are claimed. Candidates who delay logging in on the opening morning risk having only late-period dates left—or missing their preferred month entirely.

Step 3: ADC Session Review & Official Confirmation Email

Once the date selection period closes, the ADC reviews all booked sessions, sends an official confirmation email specifying your exact two-day examination dates, and updates your ADC Connect profile.

Step 4: Waiting List Mechanics (If Places Fill)

Each practical examination period maintains an electronic waiting list that opens once the application period closes. For 2027 Period 1, the ADC published four strict criteria to join the waitlist after 23 October 2026:

  1. Hold a valid initial assessment and valid written examination.
  2. Not hold a current practical examination booking or have a practical examination result pending.
  3. Not have a practical examination review or appeal pending.
  4. Agree to accept an offered examination place within the period's timeframe (the ADC notes that waitlist offers are often issued at very short notice when another candidate withdraws).

Priority Stages in 2027: What Is Confirmed vs. Unconfirmed

For 2027 Period 1 (14 – 23 October 2026), the ADC split the 10-day application period into two priority stages so candidates facing imminent document expiry could secure a seat first:

  • Stage 1 (14 – 18 October 2026): Restricted to eligible candidates (not booked in 2026 Period 3) whose valid initial assessment expired during January – May 2027 and/or whose valid written examination expired before 31 May 2027.
  • Stage 2 (19 – 23 October 2026): Opened to all remaining eligible candidates whose initial assessment and written examination expired after 31 May 2027.

Important Caution for Periods 2 and 3: As of November 17, 2026, the ADC has not yet published whether 2027 Period 2 (10 – 19 February 2027) or 2027 Period 3 (16 – 25 June 2027) will divide applications into Stage 1 and Stage 2 priority windows. Do not assume a specific day-split for February or June 2027 until the ADC publishes an official Candidate News announcement ahead of each window.

Melbourne Venue, Exam-Day ID & Results Outcome

  • Single Examination Venue: Every general dentistry practical examination is conducted at the ADC Examination Centre, Level 6, 469 La Trobe Street, Melbourne, VIC 3000, Australia. There are no international or interstate practical examination venues.
  • Mandatory Exam-Day Identification: During registration in Melbourne, you must present current, original government-issued photographic identification that includes your signature (such as your unexpired passport), and ADC staff will take your photograph on site. Review the Practical examination handbook for dentists and Candidate Rules Agreement inside ADC Connect prior to travel.
  • Results & ADC Certificate: Results are released approximately 4 to 7 months from the start of the application period. Upon passing, the ADC awards your ADC certificate (available to download in ADC Connect), which confirms your eligibility to apply for registration with the Dental Board of Australia.

Practical Candidate Scenarios for 2027

Scenario 1: You Passed the September 2026 Written Exam

  • November 2026: Receive your September 2026 written pass (after October 2026 applications for 2027 Period 1 Practical have already closed).
  • 10 – 19 February 2027: Apply for 2027 Period 2 Practical Examination and pay $4,775 AUD.
  • 23 – 25 February 2027: Select your preferred two-day practical dates during the 3-day date-selection window.
  • May – August 2027: Travel to Melbourne for your Technical Skills and Clinical Skills days.

Scenario 2: You Are Sitting the March 2027 Written Exam

  • 17 – 18 March 2027: Sit the ADC March 2027 Written Exam.
  • May 2027: Receive your written pass (~5 months after the December 2026 written application window).
  • 16 – 25 June 2027: Apply immediately for 2027 Period 3 Practical Examination, select your dates during 29 June – 1 July 2027, and sit your practical exam in Melbourne between August and December 2027. (Note: You cannot enter 2027 Period 2 Practical because its February 2027 application period closes before the March written exam occurs.)

Scenario 3: You Are Sitting the September 2027 Written Exam

  • 8 – 9 September 2027: Sit the ADC September 2027 Written Exam.
  • November 2027: Receive your written pass—after all three 2027 practical application windows have closed. Plan for the 2028 Practical Examination periods (dates not yet published by the ADC).

Common Mistakes Candidates Make with 2027 Practical Bookings

  1. Assuming Applying Guarantees Preferred Dates: Submitting your application on 10 February or 16 June 2027 only unlocks eligibility for Step 2. If you forget to log back in 48 hours after the application window closes (23 February or 29 June 2027), the date-selection window can close early once all slots are taken.
  2. Booking Non-Refundable Flights Before Session Confirmation: Do not purchase locked airfares based on a broad window like "May – August 2027". Wait until the ADC reviews the booking period and sends your official confirmation email.
  3. Overlooking Initial Assessment or Written Expiry Inside the Exam Period: Both your 7-year initial assessment and 5-year written pass must expire after your actual practical exam date—not merely on the day you apply.
  4. Joining the Waitlist Without Travel Readiness: Waitlist places open when confirmed candidates withdraw, often at very short notice. International candidates who require weeks for visa processing should only join a waitlist if they already hold a valid multiple-entry Australian visa.
  5. Practising Amalgam Restorations Instead of Contemporary Adhesive Tasks: Remember that the ADC removed the amalgam placement task from general dentistry practical examinations starting in Period 2, 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. When do applications open for the 2027 ADC practical examinations?

For general dentistry, Period 1 applications ran 14 – 23 October 2026 (closed), Period 2 applications open 10 – 19 February 2027, and Period 3 applications open 16 – 25 June 2027. Eligible candidates receive an automated email link via ADC Connect when their eligible window opens.

2. How do I choose my exact practical exam dates in 2027?

Exactly 48 hours after the application period closes, a three-day date-selection window opens in ADC Connect (23 – 25 February 2027 for Period 2; 29 June – 1 July 2027 for Period 3). You log in to select an available two-day session, keeping in mind that the window closes early if all dates are claimed.

3. Will Periods 2 and 3 in 2027 have Stage 1 and Stage 2 priority applications like Period 1?

That is currently unconfirmed. While the ADC divided 2027 Period 1 applications (14–23 October 2026) into Stage 1 (candidates with expiring initial assessments or written passes) and Stage 2 (all other eligible candidates), no stage dates have been published yet for Period 2 or Period 3.

4. Can I sit the 2027 ADC practical examination outside Melbourne?

No. All ADC practical examinations for dentists are conducted exclusively in person at the ADC Examination Centre, Level 6, 469 La Trobe Street, Melbourne, VIC 3000, Australia.

5. What if I am unsuccessful in a 2027 practical exam—how soon can I re-apply?

The current ADC practical guidance reviewed for this article does not state a separate numerical attempt cap. However, eligibility rules prevent a candidate who sat, or is due to sit, in the immediately preceding practical period from applying for the next period, and both the initial assessment and written result must remain valid. For example, a candidate who sits in 2027 Period 1 cannot apply for 2027 Period 2 and must wait until 2027 Period 3 (applying 16 – 25 June 2027).
