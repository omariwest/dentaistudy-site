ADC Written Exam Booking 2027: Pearson VUE Procedure

Booking the ADC written examination involves both ADC Connect and the ADC's Pearson examination partner. The process changed in September 2026 when the Australian Dental Council announced a trial workflow designed to let candidates choose a location before completing payment.

Quick Answers

2027 written application windows:

8–15 December 2026 for March 17–18; 8–15 June 2027 for September 8–9.

Where do you start?

In ADC Connect when the written application link appears for eligible candidates.

What changed in September 2026?

ADC announced a trial where you confirm the exam in ADC Connect, move to Pearson to choose a location, then return to ADC Connect to pay.

How long is a selected seat held under the trial?

Two hours.

Do both days need the same location?

Yes. ADC says both days must be booked at the same test centre.

Current written fee:

$2,122 AUD for general dentistry.

A successful written result is the prerequisite for ADC Practical Eligibility 2027 and later practical examination periods.

Booking the ADC written examination involves both ADC Connect and the ADC's Pearson examination partner. The process changed in September 2026 when the Australian Dental Council announced a trial workflow designed to let candidates choose a location before completing payment.

That change matters for 2027 because candidates no longer want to pay first and discover later that their preferred country or city has no seats. But there is an important caution: the ADC's current dentist written-exam page still contains parts of the older two-stage Authorisation to Test (ATT) workflow. For a 2027 booking, follow the live instructions in ADC Connect and the current ADC notice shown during your application window.

You must already hold a successful, valid initial assessment and satisfy the period's eligibility requirements before the application link becomes available.

The September 2026 Booking Change

On 23 September 2026, ADC announced a new trial with Pearson Professional Assessments.

Under the announced flow:

  1. log into ADC Connect;
  2. confirm that you want to sit the written exam;
  3. move to Pearson's booking system;
  4. choose an available exam location;
  5. return to ADC Connect;
  6. pay the examination fee.

The main benefit is that you can see and select a location before paying.

ADC said the trial was intended to become permanent if successful, but it did not guarantee that the exact process would remain unchanged for every future sitting.

That is why a 2027 candidate should use the live ADC Connect instructions rather than an old screenshot or third-party tutorial.

Why You May Still See ATT Instructions

The current ADC dentist written-exam page also describes a traditional process:

  • apply for the examination through ADC Connect;
  • receive an Authorisation to Test email from Pearson VUE later;
  • use the Pearson system during the booking period to select a venue.

This creates an apparent inconsistency between the September 2026 trial announcement and parts of the standing exam page.

The safe interpretation is straightforward: ADC Connect and the current Pearson instructions during your actual 2027 window are authoritative for that sitting.

Do not assume an old ATT timeline or the new two-hour trial flow until the live application tells you which is being used.

Step-by-Step: What to Prepare Before Booking Opens

1. Confirm written-exam eligibility

If your initial assessment is not yet complete, ADC Initial Assessment 2027 explains the current seven-year validity and submission deadlines.

Before the application period:

  • verify your initial assessment is valid through the exam dates;
  • check your passport/photo verification in ADC Connect;
  • make sure your legal name matches the identification you will present at the test centre;
  • confirm you are targeting the correct 2027 period.

2. Log in early enough to solve account problems

Do not discover a password, identity or profile issue on the final application day.

For March 2027, the application window is 8–15 December 2026.

For September 2027, it is 8–15 June 2027.

3. Choose the Pearson location carefully

ADC written examinations are delivered through Pearson test centres in Australia and selected international locations.

Seats are capacity-limited and allocated according to availability.

The ADC says candidates may sometimes need to travel outside their home country if local centres are full.

4. Book both exam days at the same centre

The general-dentistry written exam runs across two consecutive days.

ADC's 2026 booking-change notice is explicit: you must book both days at the same location. Booking only one day creates an incomplete booking and the seat can be cancelled.

5. Complete payment

The current dentist written fee and other pathway costs are summarized in ADC Exam Fees 2027.

The current general-dentistry written examination fee is $2,122 AUD.

Under the September 2026 trial, the selected Pearson seat is held for up to two hours while payment is completed.

ADC also allows a shareable payment link if another person is paying, but the same two-hour seat-hold limit applies under the trial.

If your 2027 portal instead uses the ATT workflow, follow those live payment and booking instructions.

6. Save both ADC and Pearson confirmations

Keep:

  • ADC Connect application confirmation;
  • Pearson location confirmation;
  • both examination dates;
  • exact venue address;
  • arrival instructions;
  • any candidate-rules acknowledgement.

Pearson sends a reminder before the exam, but your own confirmation should be the source of truth for travel planning.

What If Your Preferred City Is Full?

If a centre is unavailable, ADC says there are no seats remaining there at that time.

Your options are usually:

  • choose another available centre;
  • choose another city;
  • travel to another country if appropriate and feasible;
  • wait for a later written examination period if you cannot attend available locations.

Do not assume a hidden waitlist exists for a full written test centre unless ADC specifically announces one.

Can You Change the Venue?

The standing ADC written-examination page says candidates can change their Pearson VUE venue while the booking period remains open, subject to availability.

A change is not guaranteed. If the alternative centre fills, you may have to keep the existing location.

Do not cancel a workable location casually while trying to chase a more convenient one.

Withdrawal and Rescheduling

ADC examinations cannot simply be transferred to a later sitting.

If you want to move from one exam period to another, you generally need to withdraw and later submit a new application in the next application window.

The current general-dentistry written withdrawal procedure lists:

  • 80% refund before booking a Pearson venue, provided at least two weeks remain before the exam;
  • 50% refund after booking a Pearson venue, provided at least two weeks remain;
  • no refund within two weeks of the examination;
  • illness supported by medical documentation may receive a discretionary refund of no more than 50%.

Always recheck the live withdrawal procedure before acting because policy wording can change.

Same-Location Rule: Why It Matters

The dentist written examination is a two-day assessment.

Current ADC material describes four sections across the two days. Because the sitting is one examination event, ADC requires both days to be booked at the same Pearson venue.

Do not book Day 1 in one city and Day 2 elsewhere even if Pearson appears to show availability.

2027 Candidate Scenarios

Scenario 1: March candidate using the new trial

You enter ADC Connect during 8–15 December 2026, choose an available Pearson location, and are redirected back to ADC Connect. Complete the $2,122 payment within the indicated seat-hold window and save both confirmations.

Scenario 2: Your account shows the older ATT flow

Do not panic. Follow the live ADC instructions. If your application is accepted first and Pearson sends an ATT later, use that authorisation to book both March exam days at the same location.

Scenario 3: Your home-country centre is full

Check other available Pearson centres. Decide whether international travel is realistic before paying. ADC warns that some candidates may need to travel because local test-centre capacity is limited.

Scenario 4: You miss the application period

You cannot simply book directly with Pearson without an eligible ADC application. The next opportunity is the next published ADC written period.

Common Booking Mistakes

  • Using an old tutorial instead of the live ADC Connect workflow.
  • Booking only one examination day.
  • Booking different centres for Day 1 and Day 2.
  • Letting the two-hour trial seat hold expire before payment.
  • Assuming an unavailable centre will automatically create a waitlist.
  • Forgetting that written application eligibility starts with a valid initial assessment.
  • Booking non-refundable travel before the test-centre confirmation is complete.

FAQs

1. Does ADC or Pearson control the application?

Both systems are involved. Eligibility and examination application start through ADC Connect; Pearson handles test-centre booking.

2. Is the new 2026 workflow permanent?

ADC described it as a trial and said it hoped to make it permanent. Check the live 2027 workflow before assuming it is unchanged.

3. How much is the 2027 dentist written exam?

The current published fee is $2,122 AUD.

4. Can I book different test centres on the two days?

No. ADC requires both exam days at the same location.

5. What if my preferred centre is full?

Choose another available location or wait for a later sitting. ADC notes that test-centre capacity can require candidates to travel.
