ADC September 2027 Written Exam: Dates & June Applications

The ADC September 2027 written exam (officially designated by the Australian Dental Council as 2027 Written Examination Period 2 ) is the second—and final—written assessment sitting of the 2027 calendar year for internationally qualified dentists pursuing general dental registration in Australia. Because it is the last written sitting before 2028, every administrative step centres on three fixed official dates: 11 April 2027 to submit your initial assessment, 8 – 15 June 2027 to apply for your exam place, and Wednesday 8 and Thursday 9 September 2027 to sit the two-day computer-based test.

Quick Answers: September 2027 ADC Written Exam

When is the September 2027 ADC written exam?

(Official ADC Fact) The examination takes place over two consecutive weekdays: Wednesday 8 September 2027 and Thursday 9 September 2027.

What is the initial-assessment submission deadline for September 2027?

(Official ADC Fact) You must submit a complete initial assessment application on or before Sunday 11 April 2027 for your file to be assessed in time for the September 2027 sitting.

When does the written exam application window open and close?

(Official ADC Fact) Eligible candidates must apply during the eight-day window from Tuesday 8 June 2027 to Tuesday 15 June 2027.

How much is the September 2027 written exam fee?

(Official ADC Fact) The current published dentist written-examination fee is $2,122 AUD as of this article's verification date; recheck the ADC fee page before the June 2027 application window.

How long is a passing score from the September 2027 written exam valid?

(Current ADC dentist guidance) The current dentist written-exam page and June 2026 practical handbook state 5 years from notification of the successful written result. An ADC FAQ page still displays an older 3-year answer, so candidates should verify the expiry shown in ADC Connect before relying on a long-range plan.

When are September 2027 results released?

(Official ADC Fact + Calculation) The ADC states approximately 5 months from the June 2027 application period (or approximately 6 weeks after the exam), placing expected results around late October to November 2027—after all 2027 practical exam application windows have closed.

Initial-Assessment Eligibility & Validity Rules

ADC Connect enforces automated eligibility gating before issuing an application link on 8 June 2027. Review every prerequisite carefully—especially the updated validity rules linking your initial assessment and written exam. For full document checklists, see our ADC Initial Assessment & Eligibility Requirements.

1. Approved Initial Assessment & the 11 April 2027 Cutoff

The ADC Initial Assessment 2027 guide explains the document, renewal and validity issues that can determine whether the June application window is available to you.

  • Official ADC Fact: To apply for the general dentistry written examination, you must hold a successful, valid initial assessment confirming a minimum four-year full-time dental degree or diploma from a recognised provider. Because initial assessment processing takes approximately 8 weeks from receipt of a complete application, the ADC set Sunday 11 April 2027 as the final submission deadline for candidates targeting the September 2027 written exam.
  • Official ADC Fact (No Guarantee Clause): The ADC explicitly advises that submitting by 11 April 2027 is not a guarantee of examination eligibility. If your application is incomplete or requires additional verification documents, it may not be finalised before the written application window closes on 15 June 2027.
  • Calculation: Between 11 April 2027 and 8 June 2027 there are exactly 8 weeks and 2 days (58 calendar days)—leaving virtually zero margin if a single transcript or registration certificate requires clarification.
  • DentAIstudy Planning Advice: If you have not yet lodged your initial assessment, submit your complete file by mid-March 2027 so any document queries clear well before 8 June 2027. If you already hold an approved initial assessment—including if you missed the December 2026 window for the ADC March 2027 Written Exam—you do not reapply for initial assessment; your existing approval automatically unlocks the 8 – 15 June 2027 window.

2. Seven-Year Initial Assessment Validity & Pre-Expiry Renewal

  • Official ADC Fact: An approved initial assessment is valid for 7 years, and your expiry date must be later than the written examination date (9 September 2027). Crucially, the ADC states that if you have a valid written examination, it will also expire at the same time as your initial assessment.
  • Official ADC Fact (Renewal Rule): You can check your expiry date anytime on your ADC Connect dashboard and submit an initial assessment renewal ($35 AUD, registration-only check) via ADC Connect before the expiry date passes (the ADC recommends renewing within 12 months of expiry). Once an initial assessment lapses, it cannot be renewed.
  • DentAIstudy Planning Advice: Log in to ADC Connect in April or May 2027 and check your exact initial assessment expiry date. If it falls on or before 9 September 2027—or within the next 12 months—renew it before 8 June 2027.

3. Current Identification Documents in ADC Connect

  • Official ADC Fact: Before applying, your ADC Connect profile must hold current, unexpired photographic identification (such as your valid passport) and up-to-date personal details. Name changes require written notification and supporting evidence submitted to the ADC at least two weeks prior to the examination.
  • DentAIstudy Planning Advice: Check your passport expiry date in May 2027; if it expires before October 2027, renew it and update ADC Connect prior to 8 June 2027.

ADC Connect Application & Booking/Payment Procedure

Securing your seat for 8 – 9 September 2027 requires completing both your ADC Connect application and Pearson VUE / Pearson Professional Assessments test-centre booking.

Important Update on the ADC's Booking & Payment Workflow

The ADC announced a trial booking procedure on 23 September 2026 in which candidates move from ADC Connect to Pearson to choose a location and then return to ADC Connect to pay; a selected seat is held for up to two hours. However, the current dentist written-examination page still describes the older two-stage flow in which candidates first apply/pay through ADC Connect and later receive an Authorisation to Test (ATT) from Pearson VUE.

Because the September 2027 application window is still months away, follow the live instructions shown in ADC Connect and the current ADC written-exam page in June 2027. Do not assume either workflow will remain unchanged.

Step-by-Step Booking Checklist for 8 – 15 June 2027

  1. Open Your ADC Email Invitation on 8 June 2027: At the start of the 8 – 15 June 2027 period, all eligible candidates receive an automated email with a direct link to apply via ADC Connect.
  2. Verify Identification & Personal Details: Confirm your legal name matches your exam-day primary ID character-for-character and that your ID documents are current.
  3. Select Your Test Centre for Both Days: Choose an authorised Pearson VUE / Pearson Professional Assessments venue for Wednesday 8 September 2027 and Thursday 9 September 2027.
  • Hard Same-Venue Rule (Official ADC Fact): You must book the exact same test centre for both examination days. Booking only one day or splitting across two centres results in an incomplete booking and automatic cancellation.
  1. Complete the live booking/payment steps shown in ADC Connect and Pearson: If the September 2026 trial workflow is still active, location selection and payment occur in the same sequence and the seat is held for up to two hours. If ADC reverts to the ATT workflow described on its dentist written-exam page, follow the Pearson authorisation instructions instead.
  1. Save Confirmation & Watch for 7-Day Reminder: Keep your booking confirmation showing both 8 and 9 September 2027 at the identical venue address. Pearson VUE sends an automated reminder email seven days before the exam.

Delivery Locations & Written Examination Format

Delivery Locations & Test-Centre Rules (Official ADC Facts)

  • Global Pearson VUE Network: The dentist written examination is computer-based and delivered in person at authorised Pearson VUE test centres in Australia and selected international locations (remote home proctoring is not offered).
  • Workstation Capacity Limits: Individual test centres have strict seat limits allocated on a first-booked basis. If centres in your home city or country fill during the June window, no waitlist is created; you must select an available centre in another city or travel internationally to sit the exam.
  • Exam-Day Admission & Security: You must bring valid, original primary identification (such as your unexpired passport registered in ADC Connect) to both days. Pearson Professional Centres also use wand scanners during candidate admission, and calculators or electronic devices are prohibited.

The September 2027 written examination evaluates your ability to apply dental knowledge and clinical judgement safely in Australia, grounded in Australian clinical standards (such as Therapeutic Guidelines: Oral and Dental and Australian infection-control guidelines). Results Timing, 3-Year Pass Validity, Repeat & Withdrawal Rules

Results Timing & Five-Year Pass Validity

  • Results Release (~Late October – November 2027): Official ADC pages note an approximate timeline of 5 months from the June 2027 application period and approximately 6 weeks after the examination date for results to appear in ADC Connect (exact release dates are not confirmed in advance).
  • Five-Year Pass Validity (Current ADC dentist guidance): The current dentist written-examination page and the June 2026 practical handbook state that a successful written result is valid for 5 years from notification. An FAQ page still displays a 3-year answer, so use the expiry shown in ADC Connect and recheck the live dentist guidance before planning around the full five-year period.
  • Unlimited Attempts (Official ADC Fact): There is no limit on the number of times you may attempt the written examination, provided your initial assessment remains valid.

Officially Documented Rescheduling & Withdrawal Rules

Under the ADC Assessments and Examinations Withdrawal Procedure and official ADC FAQs:

  • No Rescheduling to Another Sitting: Examinations cannot be transferred or rescheduled to a future examination period. To move to a 2028 sitting, you must formally withdraw via ADC Connect and submit a new application and full fee in a future window.
  • Same-Sitting Venue Changes: You may change your Pearson VUE test centre for the September 2027 sitting only while the booking period remains open and strictly subject to seat availability.
  • Official Refund Scale (General Dentistry Written Examination):
  • Prior to booking a venue with Pearson VUE (at least 2 weeks before the exam): 80% refund (20% administration fee).
  • After booking a venue with Pearson VUE (at least 2 weeks before the exam): 50% refund.
  • Within two weeks of the examination (or late arrival / no-show): No refund (0%).
  • Withdrawal due to illness (with medical certificate): Discretionary refund of no more than 50%. Note that inability to obtain a visa or changed travel plans are treated under the standard withdrawal schedule.

Practical Planning Examples for September 2027 Candidates

A September pass usually pushes practical planning into a later cycle, so keep the current practical exam calendar and practical eligibility rules separate from assumptions about 2028 availability.

Example 1: Lodging a New Initial Assessment in Early 2027

  • Official ADC Facts: Initial assessment takes ~8 weeks; cutoff for September 2027 consideration is 11 April 2027; written applications open 8 – 15 June 2027.
  • Calculation: Submitting by 15 March 2027 gives a full 12 weeks before 8 June 2027—4 weeks more than the ADC's 8-week average processing time.
  • DentAIstudy Planning Advice: Have your dental school and licensing council send primary-source verifications in February 2027, lodge your ADC Connect initial assessment by mid-March 2027, and begin structured topic review immediately while awaiting approval.

Example 2: Re-Sitting After the March 2027 Written Exam

  • Official ADC Facts: March 2027 written exam takes place 17 – 18 March 2027, with results released ~6 weeks post-exam (~May 2027).
  • Calculation: Between receiving March 2027 results in May 2027 and the 8 – 15 June 2027 application window, you have roughly 3 to 4 weeks to review your domain cluster feedback and apply.
  • DentAIstudy Planning Advice: Because your existing initial assessment stays valid for 7 years, you do not need new academic paperwork—focus May 2027 on analysing any Grade C/D competency clusters and log in on 8 June 2027 to secure your September seat.

Example 3: What Happens If You Miss the 8–15 June 2027 Window?

  • Official ADC Facts: Once 15 June 2027 closes, no late applications or waitlists are accepted, and 8 – 9 September 2027 is the final written sitting of 2027.
  • Unconfirmed Future Dates: Official dates for 2028 Written Examination Period 1 have not yet been published by the ADC.
  • DentAIstudy Planning Advice: Keep your initial assessment active (renewing for $35 AUD if its 7-year expiry approaches), monitor ADC Candidate News in September/October 2027 for the 2028 calendar release, and use the extra months to complete two full passes of Australian therapeutic guidelines.

Realistic Progression from September 2027 Written Exam to Practical Exam

Candidates sitting the 8 – 9 September 2027 written exam must plan realistically for the final practical stage in Melbourne:

  • Why All 2027 Practical Periods Are Closed (Official ADC Fact + Calculation): Applications for 2027 Period 3 Practical Examination (the final practical period of 2027, held August – December 2027) run from 16 – 25 June 2027—nearly 11 weeks before the 8 – 9 September 2027 written exam even begins. Because you must already hold a valid written exam pass to apply for a practical place, September 2027 written candidates cannot enter any 2027 practical period.
  • Realistic 2028 Practical Progression (Unconfirmed Dates): With September 2027 written results expected around late October to November 2027, your earliest practical opportunities fall in 2028 Practical Examination periods at the ADC Examination Centre in Melbourne (exact 2028 application and testing dates are unconfirmed). Note also that once you pass in September 2027, your 5-year written exam validity means you must complete your practical examination before late 2032 (and keep your 7-year initial assessment renewed if it expires earlier). Common Mistakes September 2027 Candidates Make

  1. Using an older three-year rule without checking current dentist guidance: The current dentist written-exam page and June 2026 practical handbook state 5 years. Because one ADC FAQ still shows 3 years, verify the expiry displayed in ADC Connect.
  2. Lodging an Initial Assessment on 11 April 2027 With Missing Documents: Submitting on the final deadline leaves zero buffer if university verification delays approval past 15 June 2027.
  3. Waiting Until 15 June 2027 to Apply: High-demand Pearson VUE centres in Australia and major overseas cities reach workstation capacity rapidly; delaying until the final day risks having to travel interstate or internationally.
  4. Booking Different Test Centres for Day 1 and Day 2: Both 8 September and 9 September 2027 must be booked at the identical Pearson VUE venue.
  5. Overlooking Cluster-Based Grading: Passing requires achieving Grade A or B in all four competency clusters—a strong score in restorative dentistry will not compensate for a failing grade in professionalism/health promotion or clinical information gathering.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the initial-assessment submission deadline for the September 2027 ADC written exam?

(Official ADC Fact) You must submit a complete initial assessment application on or before Sunday 11 April 2027. However, your file must be finalised and approved before the written application window closes on 15 June 2027, so submitting several weeks earlier is strongly advised.

2. Can I apply after 15 June 2027 if spots are still open?

(Official ADC Fact) No. The 8 – 15 June 2027 application period is strictly enforced with no late entries or waitlists. If you miss 15 June 2027, your next opportunity is 2028 Written Examination Period 1 (official 2028 dates are currently unconfirmed).

3. How long is my September 2027 ADC written exam pass valid?

(Official ADC Fact) Under current ADC guidance, a passing result in the dentist written examination is valid for 5 years. If your underlying 7-year initial assessment expires earlier, your written exam pass expires with it unless you renew your initial assessment prior to its expiry date.

4. Can I take the September 2027 written exam outside Australia?

(Official ADC Fact) Yes. The computer-based written examination is delivered at authorised Pearson VUE test centres in Australia and selected international cities. Because centres have workstation limits, you may need to travel to another city or country if local seats fill early.

5. When is the earliest I can sit the practical exam if I pass in September 2027?

(Official ADC Fact + Calculation) Because September 2027 written results are released around late October to November 2027—after all 2027 practical application periods have closed—your earliest practical examination opportunities will be in the 2028 Practical Examination periods in Melbourne (exact 2028 dates are unconfirmed).
