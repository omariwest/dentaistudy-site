ADC March 2027 Written Exam: Dates, Eligibility & Application Deadline

The ADC March 2027 written exam (officially designated by the Australian Dental Council as 2027 Written Examination Period 1 ) is the first of only two written assessment sittings held in 2027 for internationally qualified dentists pursuing general dental registration in Australia. Because the ADC enforces a strict eight-day application window in December 2026—paired with updated venue-booking and payment rules introduced in late 2026—understanding the exact sequence of deadlines is essential to avoid losing a six-month cycle.

Quick Answers: March 2027 ADC Written Exam

  • When is the March 2027 ADC written exam? The examination takes place across two consecutive weekdays: Wednesday 17 March 2027 and Thursday 18 March 2027.
  • When is the application window for the March 2027 exam? Eligible candidates must apply between Tuesday 8 December 2026 and Tuesday 15 December 2026.
  • Can I still submit a new initial assessment for the March 2027 sitting? No. The official initial-assessment submission deadline for March 2027 eligibility was 11 October 2026. Only candidates who already hold an approved initial assessment—or who submitted a complete application on or before 11 October 2026 and receive approval before 15 December 2026—can enter the March 2027 sitting.
  • How much is the March 2027 written exam fee? Following the ADC's 12-month fee freeze announced in June 2026, the published written examination fee is $2,122 AUD.
  • How does venue booking work for March 2027? Under the ADC's updated booking trial developed with Pearson Professional Assessments, test-centre selection and fee payment happen together at the same time (with a two-hour reservation window to complete payment), and you must book both exam days at the exact same venue.
  • When are March 2027 results released? The ADC estimates approximately 5 months from the December 2026 application period to results release—placing expected results around May 2027, in time to apply for 2027 Period 3 Practical Examination (16–25 June 2027).

Eligibility & Initial Assessment Requirements

If your assessment status is still unclear, use ADC Initial Assessment 2027 before making a booking plan.

The ADC enforces strict automated eligibility checks before issuing an application invitation on 8 December 2026. Meeting the academic standard alone is not enough—your administrative status and document expiry dates must remain valid through the final day of the examination.

1. Approved Initial Assessment Status

To sit the written examination, you must hold a successful initial assessment confirming that you hold a minimum four-year full-time dental degree/diploma and meet ADC eligibility criteria. Because initial assessments carry an approximate 8-week processing timeframe from receipt of a complete application, the ADC set 11 October 2026 as the final submission cutoff for candidates targeting March 2027.

  • Already Approved: If your initial assessment is already finalised and valid, you will receive an automated email link when the application window opens on 8 December 2026.
  • Submitted On or Before 11 October 2026 (Pending Outcome): You can only enter the March 2027 sitting if the ADC completes your assessment and marks you eligible before the application window closes on 15 December 2026. The ADC explicitly notes that submitting by 11 October 2026 does not guarantee eligibility if primary-source verifications or additional documents are still outstanding.
  • Not Yet Submitted: You cannot apply for the March 2027 written exam. Review our ADC Initial Assessment & Eligibility Requirements and aim for the 11 April 2027 cutoff for the September 2027 written exam.

2. Seven-Year Validity & Pre-Expiry Renewal Rule

An approved ADC initial assessment remains valid for 7 years from the date of assessment outcome. However, ADC eligibility rules require that your initial assessment expiry date be later than the written examination date (18 March 2027).

If your initial assessment was approved in early 2020 and will expire before 18 March 2027, you must apply for an initial assessment renewal ($35 AUD, registration-only check) via ADC Connect before the seven-year expiry date passes. Once an initial assessment expires, it cannot be renewed—you would have to submit a full new initial assessment application ($647 AUD) and forfeit any previously passed written examination.

3. Valid Passport & Identity Matching

Your ADC Connect profile must reflect current, unexpired government photo identification (typically your passport) whose legal name matches your exam registration character-for-character. If your passport expires before 18 March 2027, renew it and update your ADC Connect profile ahead of 8 December 2026.

Step-by-Step Application & Venue Booking Procedure

The separate ADC Written Booking 2027 guide covers the current Pearson/ADC Connect flow in more detail, while ADC Exam Fees 2027 keeps the application and examination costs in one place.

The ADC updated its written examination booking workflow in September 2026 after consultation with candidates and its written examination delivery partner, Pearson Professional Assessments. Understanding both the official portal rules and this updated booking flow is critical so you do not lose a reserved seat during checkout.

How the Updated Booking & Payment Workflow Operates

Previously, candidates applied and paid through ADC Connect first, then waited weeks for a separate Pearson VUE Authorisation to Test (ATT) email before discovering whether seats were available in their preferred city. Under the ADC's trial booking procedure announced on 23 September 2026:

  1. Receive Your Invitation Email (8 December 2026): At the start of the 8 – 15 December 2026 application period, all eligible candidates receive an email with a direct link to apply.
  2. Select Your Test Centre Location & Both Exam Days Together: Instead of paying before seeing seat availability, location booking and fee payment happen at the same time. You must select the same test centre location for both Wednesday 17 March 2027 and Thursday 18 March 2027. Selecting only one day creates an incomplete booking and cancels your seat.
  3. Complete Payment Within the Two-Hour Hold ($2,122 AUD): Once you select your seats for both days, the system reserves those workstations for two hours. If payment is not completed within that two-hour limit, your seats are automatically released back into the public pool for other candidates to book.
  4. Optional Shared Payment Link: If a family member or sponsor is paying on your behalf, the booking screen provides a copyable payment link you can send to another person—the same two-hour payment countdown still applies.
  5. Confirm Your Booking Receipt: Once payment clears, verify that your confirmation notice lists both 17 March and 18 March 2027 at the identical Pearson Professional Assessments / Pearson VUE venue address.

(Note: The ADC stated in its 23 September 2026 announcement that this combined location-and-payment workflow is currently operating as a trial that it aims to make permanent if successful. Always follow the exact instructions inside your 8 December 2026 ADC invitation email.)

Delivery Locations, Exam Format & Post-Exam Policies

Delivery Locations & Seat Capacity Rules

  • Global Test-Centre Network: The written examination is delivered via computer at authorised Pearson VUE / Pearson Professional Assessments test centres in Australia and selected international locations.
  • No Waitlists at Full Centres: Seats at each physical test centre are capped by workstation availability. If your preferred city shows no available seats during 8 – 15 December 2026, all seats at that venue are full, and you must either select another available city/country or wait for a future sitting.
  • Strict Exam-Day ID Enforcement: You must bring the exact original, unexpired passport/ID registered on your profile to both exam sessions. Digital copies or expired documents result in denied entry without a refund.

Written Examination Format (Planning Overview)

The March 2027 written examination evaluates whether you "know how" to practise dentistry safely in Australia, testing applied dental science, clinical judgement, and reasoning aligned with Australian clinical standards (including Therapeutic Guidelines: Oral and Dental and Australian infection-control guidelines). Results Timeframe, Pass Validity, Repeat Rules & Withdrawals

  • Results Timeframe (~May 2027): The ADC estimates approximately 5 months from the application period (December 2026) to results release. Exact release dates are not published in advance; notifications appear directly in ADC Connect.
  • five-year Pass Validity: A passing result in the written examination remains valid for 5 years, provided your initial assessment also stays valid (or is renewed prior to its 7-year expiry).
  • Unlimited Attempts: There is no limit on the number of times you may attempt the written examination. If unsuccessful in March 2027, you can apply during 8 – 15 June 2027 for the 8 – 9 September 2027 sitting.
  • Venue Changes & Withdrawals: Once booked, test-centre location changes are only possible while the booking window remains open and are strictly subject to seat availability. Formal withdrawal rules and any applicable administrative fee deductions are set out in the Written handbook for general dentistry and the candidate terms accepted during booking. (Note: While the ADC introduced an updated withdrawal refund scale in June 2026 for dental hygiene, dental therapy, and dental prosthetics written exams, general dentistry written exam candidates must check the current Written handbook for general dentistry for exact general-dentistry withdrawal terms.)

Practical Candidate Scenarios

After a successful written result, use ADC Practical Exam Dates 2027 together with ADC Practical Eligibility 2027 to decide which later period is realistically available.

Scenario 1: Eligible Candidate Booking on 8 December 2026

  • Late November 2026: Confirm your initial assessment status in ADC Connect, verify your passport expiry date, and prepare a payment method cleared for $2,122 AUD.
  • 8 December 2026: Open your ADC invitation email immediately upon arrival, select both 17 March and 18 March 2027 at your preferred test centre, and complete payment within the 2-hour reservation window.
  • 17 – 18 March 2027: Sit Papers 1–4.
  • May – June 2027: Receive your passing result (~5 months from December application) and prepare to apply during 16 – 25 June 2027 for 2027 Period 3 Practical Examination.

Scenario 2: Candidate Re-Sitting After September 2026

  • November 2026: Review your September 2026 domain feedback report upon release to pinpoint weak clinical areas.
  • 8 – 15 December 2026: Log in via your ADC invitation email (no new initial assessment is required while your 7-year validity remains active), book both March 2027 exam days at the same centre, and pay $2,122 AUD within 2 hours.

Scenario 3: What If You Miss the 8–15 December 2026 Window?

Once 15 December 2026 closes, there is no late registration or waitlist for the March 2027 written exam. If you miss the December window—or if your initial assessment submitted by 11 October 2026 is not finalised before 15 December 2026—pivot immediately to 2027 Written Examination Period 2:

  • Initial Assessment Submission Deadline: 11 April 2027
  • Written Exam Application Window: 8 – 15 June 2027
  • Examination Dates: Wednesday 8 and Thursday 9 September 2027

Realistic Progression from March 2027 Written Exam to a 2027 Practical Exam

Many candidates assume that passing the written exam in March 2027 allows them to sit the practical exam in May or June 2027. Under the confirmed 2027 ADC calendar, that timeline is impossible:

  • Why 2027 Period 2 Practical (May – August 2027) Is Closed to March Written Candidates: Applications for 2027 Period 2 Practical Examination open 10 – 19 February 2027 (with date selection 23 – 25 February 2027)—nearly a month before the 17–18 March 2027 written exam begins. Because an existing written exam pass is mandatory to apply for the practical exam, March 2027 written candidates cannot apply in February.
  • Your Realistic 2027 Target — 2027 Period 3 Practical Examination (August – December 2027):
  • May 2027: Receive your March 2027 written exam pass notification.
  • 16 – 25 June 2027: Apply for 2027 Period 3 Practical Examination.
  • 29 June – 1 July 2027: Log in 48 hours after applications close to claim your two-day practical exam slot during the date selection period (which closes early if all dates fill).
  • August – December 2027: Travel to the ADC Examination Centre in Melbourne, Victoria to sit your Technical Skills and Clinical Skills days.
  • Important Caveat on Practical Seat Demand: In October 2026 (for 2027 Period 1 Practical), the ADC divided practical applications into priority stages—giving Stage 1 access to candidates whose initial assessment or written pass was expiring soon—before opening Stage 2 to remaining candidates. If similar staging applies in June 2027 and Period 3 fills before you secure a date, your next opportunity will be 2028 Period 1 Practical Examination (dates not yet published by ADC). For full details on practical periods, read ADC Practical Exam Dates 2027.

Common Mistakes March 2027 Candidates Make

  1. Letting the Two-Hour Seat Hold Expire: Under the updated booking system, selecting your preferred centre does not lock it permanently—if payment fails or takes longer than 2 hours, the system releases your seats back into the pool.
  2. Attempting to Book Only One Day or Two Different Venues: Selecting only 17 March or pairing two separate test centres causes an incomplete booking and automatic seat cancellation. Both 17 March and 18 March 2027 must be booked at the identical centre.
  3. Waiting Until 15 December 2026 to Apply: High-demand cities in Australia and major international hubs fill early during the 8-day window. Waiting until the final day leaves zero margin if your card declines or preferred venue sells out.
  4. Overlooking a Seven-Year Initial Assessment Expiry Date: Candidates who completed their initial assessment years ago sometimes check only their 5-year written validity and miss that their 7-year initial assessment expires before 18 March 2027.
  5. Revising Non-Australian Clinical Guidelines: Relying on North American or European antibiotic protocols, local anaesthetic maximum doses, or recall intervals leads to avoidable errors on Australian clinical scenarios.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can I still apply for the March 2027 ADC written exam if I haven't submitted my initial assessment yet?

No. The official initial-assessment submission deadline for the March 2027 written examination was 11 October 2026. If you have not yet submitted your initial assessment, submit a complete application on or before 11 April 2027 to target the 8 – 9 September 2027 written examination instead.

2. Why do location booking and payment happen together for the March 2027 exam?

Following candidate feedback, the ADC and Pearson Professional Assessments introduced a trial booking workflow where location selection and fee payment occur simultaneously. This prevents candidates from paying the $2,122 AUD fee only to discover weeks later that no seats remain in their preferred city.

3. How long do I have to pay once I select my March 2027 test centre?

Your selected seats for 17 and 18 March 2027 are reserved for two hours. If payment is not completed within two hours (either by you or via a shared payment link), the reservation expires and the seats return to the general pool.

4. What happens if I fail the March 2027 written exam—is there an attempt limit?

There is no limit on the number of times you can attempt the ADC written examination. Provided your initial assessment remains valid (within its 7-year validity window or renewed prior to expiry), you can re-apply during 8 – 15 June 2027 for the 8 – 9 September 2027 sitting.

5. If I pass the March 2027 written exam, when is the earliest I can take the practical exam?

Because March 2027 results are expected approximately five months after the December 2026 application period (around May 2027), your earliest practical examination opportunity is 2027 Period 3, which opens for applications from 16 – 25 June 2027 for testing in Melbourne between August and December 2027.
